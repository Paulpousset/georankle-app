Spain

Four neighbours, one of them across the Strait of Gibraltar.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Spain has 48.8 million people according to the game, thirty-second in the world, on 505,976 km², fifty-third by area. Its density, about 97 people per square kilometre, is moderate: the interior plateau is thinly populated, and people gather on the coast and around Madrid.

The capital is Madrid, in the exact centre of the peninsula, also the largest city. There is no capital trap, but one common question: Barcelona, the second city, is not the capital. Madrid is also one of the highest capitals in Europe, at over 600 metres.

The game lists four land neighbours: Portugal to the west, France and Andorra to the north and, more surprisingly, Morocco. The Moroccan border really does exist on land, around Ceuta and Melilla, two Spanish cities on the African coast. Gibraltar, a British territory, is not a country in the game.

The flag is made of two red bands framing a wide yellow band, with the coat of arms on the left. Do not confuse it with Portugal’s, which is green and red.
