Japan

An archipelago with no land border, denser than Germany and with nearly half as many people again.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Japan has 124 million people according to the game, twelfth in the world, between Mexico and Egypt. Its 378,000 km² rank only sixty-second by area, barely more than Germany. The result is a density of 328 people per square kilometre, higher than most large countries: people crowd along the coasts, because the interior is mostly mountains.

The capital is Tokyo, also the largest city in the country. That is not always so: the imperial capital was Kyoto for centuries, and the seat of power moved to Tokyo in 1868. The name means “eastern capital”.

Japan has no land neighbours, like all island nations: it is a chain of islands whose four main ones are Honshu, Hokkaido, Kyushu and Shikoku. In the Borders mode it simply does not appear. Its closest neighbours lie across the sea: South Korea, China and Russia.

The flag is a red disc on a white field. It is the easiest flag to describe, and that is what makes it treacherous in a quiz. Bangladesh also has a red disc, but on green and shifted towards the hoist; Palau has a yellow disc on blue. Japan’s disc is centred and the background white.
