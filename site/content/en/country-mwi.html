Malawi

A small, narrow country, very dense by African standards, whose entire eastern frontier is a lake.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Malawi has 21.7 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 61st in the world, on only 118,480 km², the 100th territory on the list. Density therefore reaches about 183 people per square kilometre, one of the highest on the continent: the country is narrow, stretched from north to south, and almost entirely rural.

The capital is Lilongwe, in the centre, not Blantyre, older and more commercial, which many players type by reflex. Malawi is landlocked and has just three neighbours: Tanzania to the north, Zambia to the west, and Mozambique wrapping around it to the south and east. On a map the country looks like a finger poking between them.

Its most recognisable feature is Lake Malawi, called Lake Nyasa in Tanzania and Mozambique, which fills much of the eastern frontier. For visual memory, keep this: Malawi is the country “of the lake”.

The flag has three horizontal bands, black, red and green, with a red rising sun on the black band: the sun rising over Africa. Kenya uses the same colours, but with white fringes and a shield in the centre; in Malawi, the sun is the only figure.
