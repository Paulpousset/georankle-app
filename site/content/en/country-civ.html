Ivory Coast

Yamoussoukro is the capital, Abidjan the metropolis: the classic trick question.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Ivory Coast, known locally as Côte d’Ivoire, has 31.9 million people according to the game, fiftieth in the world, on 322,460 km², the sixty-eighth largest territory. Density reaches about 99 people per square kilometre, lower than neighbouring Ghana.

The game’s capital is Yamoussoukro, in the centre of the country. The largest city and economic hub is Abidjan, on the coast, which most players name by instinct. That is the trick question: the official capital moved to Yamoussoukro in 1983, but most institutions and business stayed in Abidjan. For a quiz the expected answer is Yamoussoukro, and the game follows that. The same kind of split exists in Bolivia, South Africa and the Netherlands.

The game gives it five land neighbours: Ghana to the east, Burkina Faso and Mali to the north, Guinea and Liberia to the west. To the south the country faces the Gulf of Guinea. All five neighbours are in West Africa; Ghana and Liberia speak English, the other three French, which helps to remember them as two groups.

The flag has three vertical bands, orange, white and green. It is the mirror image of Ireland’s: green, white, orange. A simple trick to keep them apart: on the Ivorian flag the orange is at the hoist, on the left; for Ireland it is the green. The detail is in the guide to similar flags.
