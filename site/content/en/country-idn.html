Indonesia

An archipelago with three land neighbours and a capital on the move.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

Indonesia is the fourth country in the world by population, with 283 million people according to the game, and the country with the most Muslims. It is also an archipelago whose islands stretch over several thousand kilometres from east to west; it ranks fourteenth by area.

The game's capital is Jakarta, on the island of Java, where a considerable share of the country's population lives. This one needs watching: Indonesia has decided to move its capital to Nusantara on the island of Borneo, a project launched in the 2020s. Jakarta suffers from sinking ground and heavy congestion. As long as the game and your sources say Jakarta, that is the answer to give; it is useful to know Nusantara for current-affairs questions.

Indonesia has only three playable land neighbours: Malaysia and Papua New Guinea, which share the islands of Borneo and New Guinea with it, and Timor-Leste, on the island of Timor. In Borders mode these are three surprising answers, because one does not think of an island country as having land neighbours.

One last point for a flag quiz: Indonesia's flag, red over white, is almost Monaco's. The only difference is the proportions of the rectangle.
