Netherlands

A small, flat territory partly reclaimed from the sea, home to nearly 18 million people: the densest country in Europe once the micro-states are set aside.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

The Netherlands has nearly 18 million inhabitants according to the game data, placing it 71st in the world and tenth in Europe. Its area, 41,540 km², is only the 131st on the list: density exceeds 430 people per square kilometre. In Europe, only the Vatican, Malta, San Marino and Monaco are more crowded, and none of them is the size of a real country.

The capital is Amsterdam, and that is the answer the quiz expects. The government, parliament and embassies nevertheless sit in The Hague, which earns the Netherlands a place in the guide to countries with several capitals. Rotterdam, the great port, is the third city players wrongly suggest.

The country has just two neighbours: Belgium to the south and Germany to the east. Everything else is coast, facing the North Sea. A good part of the territory lies below sea level, protected by dykes and polders: that is the detail that makes the country’s outline hard to recognise, because its coastline has been redrawn by hand, with the large enclosed basins of the IJsselmeer in the centre.

The flag is a horizontal tricolour of red, white and blue. Its lookalike is Luxembourg’s, whose blue is lighter and whose shape is more elongated. Russia uses the same three colours in another order, white on top.
