Cambodia

Wedged between Thailand and Vietnam, Cambodia is the country of the Mekong, of the great Tonlé Sap lake and of the only flag in the game where you can recognise a temple.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

With 17.6 million inhabitants according to the game data, Cambodia is the 72nd country in the world by population and the seventh of the eleven countries of South-East Asia. Its area of 181,040 km², ranked 88th, is close to that of Uruguay or Senegal; density is around 97 people per square kilometre.

The capital, Phnom Penh, lies at the confluence of the Mekong and the Tonlé Sap, the river that links the great lake of the same name to the Mekong. That lake is the most useful landmark on a map: a patch of water in the centre-west of the country that swells every year in the rainy season. Siem Reap, the town near the temples of Angkor, is not the capital, but it is the name many travellers remember.

Cambodia has three neighbours: Thailand to the west and north, Laos to the north, Vietnam to the east and south-east. It has a coastline on the Gulf of Thailand, to the south-west, which sets it apart from Laos, the only landlocked country in the region.

The flag is unmistakable: two blue bands frame a wide red band bearing, in white, the outline of Angkor Wat. It is one of the very few national flags to depict a building, which also makes it one of the easiest to remember once seen.
