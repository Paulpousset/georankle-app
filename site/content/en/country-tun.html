Tunisia

The smallest of the Maghreb countries is also the one that reaches furthest towards Europe: its northern tip is the northernmost point of the African continent.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Tunisia has 12.3 million inhabitants according to the game data, 79th in the world and 31st in Africa, on 163,610 km², the 91st territory on the list. Of Africa’s 54 countries, only nineteen are smaller. Density, about 75 people per square kilometre, hides a sharp contrast between a fertile north and an almost empty Saharan south.

The capital is Tunis, at the head of a Mediterranean gulf, next to the ruins of Carthage. The name of the capital and that of the country are so close that there is no trap here: it is one of the safest answers in the North Africa capitals quiz.

The country has only two neighbours, Algeria to the west and Libya to the south-east, for a long Mediterranean coast facing Sicily. On the globe, look for the small country squeezed between two giants: Algeria, the largest country in Africa by area, and Libya, the fourth. Tunisia is nearly fifteen times smaller than its western neighbour but almost as populous as Libya and Mauritania put together.

The flag is red with, in the centre, a white disc containing a red crescent and star. Turkey places a white crescent and star directly on the red field: the presence of the white disc is the detail that separates the two, covered in the guide to lookalike flags.
