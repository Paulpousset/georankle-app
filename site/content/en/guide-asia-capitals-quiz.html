Asia capitals quiz: 47 capitals, five blocks and eleven special cases

In Asia the difficulty is not only remembering names, it is knowing which answer the quiz expects when the status of a capital is debated. Here are the blocks, then the tricky cases.

Guide · game data, October 2026

The game has {{count:asie}} countries in Asia, Turkey, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan and Kazakhstan included, hence as many capitals. The famous relocations (Naypyidaw, Astana, Ankara) are told in the list of Asian capitals. This page goes at it differently: it splits the continent into study blocks, then reviews the eleven countries whose capital is less simple than it looks.

Five blocks, one very large

The game data splits Asia into five subregions. Western Asia holds seventeen on its own, Caucasus and Middle East included, which makes it the longest block to learn. In each cell the capitals are ordered from the most to the least populous country.

Eastern and Central Asia have only five countries each: they are the two fastest blocks. Western Asia splits naturally in three: the Arabian peninsula, the Levant, and the Caucasus with its neighbours.

When the answer depends on who gives it

Israel and Palestine. The status of Jerusalem is the subject of an international dispute. The game uses Jerusalem for Israel, like most quizzes, and Ramallah, seat of the Palestinian Authority, for Palestine. The second answer surprises people: the State of Palestine also claims Jerusalem.

Sri Lanka. Colombo is the city everyone names, but the capital is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, seat of parliament, in the suburbs of Colombo. The expected answer is the long name.

Malaysia. The game uses Kuala Lumpur. Putrajaya, a city built to house the administration, is the seat of government; some sources also call it the “administrative capital”.

Indonesia. The game uses Jakarta. A project for a new capital, Nusantara, on the island of Borneo, has been under construction since a 2022 law; as long as the move is not recorded in the game data, the right answer remains Jakarta. Worth checking if you read this page in a few years.

The big cities that are not capitals

  • United Arab Emirates: Abu Dhabi, not Dubai, which is more populous and better known.
  • Pakistan: Islamabad, a city built in the twentieth century to replace Karachi, which remains the country’s largest city.
  • Turkey: Ankara, not Istanbul.
  • India: New Delhi, not Mumbai or Calcutta.
  • Myanmar: Naypyidaw, not Yangon.
  • Kazakhstan: Astana, not Almaty, the capital until 1997.

Two families of names not to mix up

The five “-stan” countries of Central Asia: Astana (Kazakhstan), Tashkent (Uzbekistan), Bishkek (Kyrgyzstan), Dushanbe (Tajikistan) and Ashgabat (Turkmenistan). Five neighbouring countries with similar names and five capitals with similar sounds: you only remember them well by placing them on a map.

The five “D”s: Dhaka (Bangladesh), Damascus (Syria), Dili (Timor-Leste), Doha (Qatar) and Dushanbe (Tajikistan). They are scattered across the continent and share only the initial: in a four-option question this is the kind of series that makes people hesitate, and it is better to remember the country than the letter.

A three-session plan

  1. Session 1: East and Central Asia. Ten countries, with the five “-stan” capitals.
  2. Session 2: South and South-East Asia. Twenty countries, to split in two, handling Sri Lanka, Malaysia and Indonesia separately.
  3. Session 3: Western Asia. Seventeen countries, in three subgroups, with Israel and Palestine last. Finish with a full round of all {{count:asie}} countries.

Space the sessions two or three days apart, as the study guide recommends.

Frequently asked questions

How many capitals does Asia have in the game?
{{count:asie}}, one per country. The game files Turkey, Kazakhstan and the three Caucasus countries under Asia, and Russia under Europe.
What is the capital of Palestine in the game?
Ramallah, seat of the Palestinian Authority. The State of Palestine also claims Jerusalem, whose status is disputed.
Why is the capital of Sri Lanka not Colombo?
Because parliament sits in Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, in the suburbs of Colombo. Colombo remains the largest city and the commercial centre.
Dubai or Abu Dhabi?
Abu Dhabi. Dubai is better known and more populous, but Abu Dhabi is the capital of the United Arab Emirates.
