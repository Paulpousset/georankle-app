Poland

Warsaw rather than Kraków, and a two-band flag that two other countries share.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Poland has 36.6 million people according to the game, forty-second in the world. Its 313,930 km² put it sixty-ninth by area, which works out at about 117 people per square kilometre: a fairly dense country by world standards, and by far the most populous of the Central Europe the game defines, alongside Austria, Czechia, Hungary, Slovakia and Slovenia.

The capital is Warsaw. Players who hesitate think of Kraków, the former royal capital and a magnet for tourists, but the title moved to Warsaw at the end of the sixteenth century. A handy association: Warsaw stands on the Vistula in the east-centre of the country, far from the southern mountains.

The game gives it seven land neighbours, which is a lot: Germany to the west, Czechia and Slovakia to the south, Ukraine and Belarus to the east, Lithuania to the north-east and Russia to the north, through the Kaliningrad exclave. That last border is the one that costs points, because Poland touches Russia without the two looking like obvious neighbours on a world map. On the north coast, the country faces the Baltic Sea.

The flag is a white band over a red band. It is identical to Monaco’s apart from proportions, and it flips into Indonesia’s, red above white. A memory aid: in Poland the white sits on top, like snow over red; the guide to similar flags goes through all three cases.
