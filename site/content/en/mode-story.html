How it works

Story mode is a campaign of {{storyLevels}} levels laid out on a map you climb level by level. Each level offers a handful of questions drawn from the game's modes, and earns one to three stars depending on your score. You need a minimum of stars to continue, never perfection.

Difficulty that follows familiarity, not chance

That is what sets the mode apart from a mere sequence of games. GeoG ranks its {{countries}} countries by familiarity, and the campaign follows that scale: the first levels draw from countries everyone knows, the last from countries almost nobody can place. Progression is steady, with a wide enough band that each level stays varied.

In practice, level 1 will ask you for France or Brazil; level {{storyLevels}}, for Kiribati or Turkmenistan.

Lives, and ways to get them back

Failing a level costs a life. Lives regenerate over time, and you can recover one immediately — it is the only place in the game where an advert is offered, never imposed.

Offline

Story mode works without a connection in the mobile app: the entire campaign is derived from the level number, with no network call. It is the format to take on a plane or the underground.

Difficulty, continent by continent

The last levels draw heavily on Oceania and the Caribbean: those are the areas that concentrate the least familiar countries, and where the number of states bears no relation to their population.

Frequently asked

How many levels does story mode have?
{{storyLevels}}, from the best known to the most obscure. Difficulty follows how familiar the countries are, not a random draw.
Is story mode free?
Entirely. Lives regenerate over time; an optional advert lets you get one back immediately, but it is never imposed.
Can I play offline?
Yes, in the mobile app: the whole campaign is derived from the level number, with no network call.
Do I need three stars to move on?
No. A minimum threshold unlocks the next level; the three stars are there for players who want to finish everything perfectly.
