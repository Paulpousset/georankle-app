Burkina Faso

A landlocked country that changed its name in 1984, six neighbours and a capital to spell: Ouagadougou.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Burkina Faso has 23.5 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 59th in the world, over 274,200 km², the 73rd largest territory on the planet. Its density reaches about 86 people per square kilometre, markedly more than its large neighbour Mali: the country is smaller and more populated.

The capital is Ouagadougou, in the centre of the country, a classic of capital quizzes: long, with several “ou” sounds, it sticks in the memory precisely because it is unusual. The country is landlocked and has six neighbours: Mali to the north-west, Niger to the east, Benin, Togo and Ghana to the south-east and south, Ivory Coast to the south-west.

It was called Upper Volta until 1984, a name borrowed from the Volta river tributaries that cross it. The current name, Burkina Faso, blends two local languages and is often translated as “land of upright people”. It is one of the continent’s most recent name changes, covered in the guide to countries that changed name.

The flag has two horizontal bands, red above and green below, with a yellow star at the centre.
