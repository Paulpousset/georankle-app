Philippines

An archipelago of thousands of islands, no land border, and one of the highest densities among big countries.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

The Philippines have 116 million people according to the game: fourteenth in the world by population, just behind Egypt. Its 300,000 km² rank seventy-second by area. Density reaches 386 people per square kilometre; among the fifteen most populous countries, only Bangladesh and India are denser.

The capital is Manila, on Luzon, the largest island in the archipelago. There is no capital trap here: it is the name you would expect. The difficulty is geographic, since the archipelago has over seven thousand islands, and the game never asks you to name them all.

Like Japan, the country has no land neighbours. Its neighbours are across the water: Taiwan to the north, Malaysia and Indonesia to the south, Vietnam to the west across the South China Sea. To place it on a map, look for big Luzon in the north, Mindanao in the south, and the cluster of the Visayas in between.

The flag has two bands, blue on top and red below, with a white triangle at the hoist holding a sun and three stars. It has a feature found nowhere else: in wartime it is flown upside down, with the red band on top.
