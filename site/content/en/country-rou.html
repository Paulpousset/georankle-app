Romania

An arc of Carpathians, a Danube delta and a flag that is a twin of Chad’s.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Romania has 19.1 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 66th in the world, on 238,400 km², the 81st territory on the list. Density reaches about 80 people per square kilometre, with a population denser on the southern and eastern plains than in the mountains.

The capital is Bucharest, in the south, on the plain. The game counts five neighbours: Hungary to the west, Serbia to the south-west, Bulgaria to the south, Moldova to the east and Ukraine to the north. The country is coastal: it opens onto the Black Sea, and the Danube, which marks much of its southern border, ends its course there in a vast delta.

Geographically, Romania is organised around the Carpathian arc, which separates Transylvania, inland, from the plains of Wallachia and Moldavia. In a placement quiz, you find it by spotting the country whose shape suggests a circle closed by the Black Sea to the east.

The flag has three vertical bands of blue, yellow and red: it is the near-perfect twin of Chad’s, and it also resembles that of neighbouring Moldova, which adds a coat of arms. When in doubt in a quiz, the answer depends on the other options: a European country, it is Romania.
