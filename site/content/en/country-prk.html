North Korea

Only three neighbours, including China and Russia, and a peninsula shared with South Korea.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

North Korea has 26.5 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 56th in the world, over 120,540 km², the 99th largest territory. Density reaches about 220 people per square kilometre, higher than many much larger countries.

The capital is Pyongyang, in the west of the country, inland. The country occupies the northern half of a peninsula that juts into the sea between the Yellow Sea to the west and the Sea of Japan to the east.

It has three land neighbours in the game: China, along most of the northern border, Russia, along a very short stretch in the far north-east, and South Korea to the south. The border with South Korea follows a demilitarised zone, and it is the line hardest to fix from memory on a blank map.

To avoid swapping the two Koreas, remember the direction: the North is the one that touches China and Russia, the South the one at the tip of the peninsula, facing Japan. Their capitals, Pyongyang and Seoul, follow the same logic: the more northerly is Pyongyang. The South Korea profile extends this landmark.
