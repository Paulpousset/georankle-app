South Africa

Three capitals, a country entirely enclosed within it, and the same world rank for population and for area.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

South Africa has 64.0 million people according to the game, twenty-fourth in the world, and 1,219,090 km², also twenty-fourth by area. The double twenty-fourth place is an easy curiosity to remember. The density of 53 people per square kilometre is moderate.

The capital question has three answers: Pretoria for the executive, Cape Town for the legislature, Bloemfontein for the judiciary. The game uses Pretoria, the seat of government. It is the only case on the continent where three cities share the title, an arrangement inherited from the 1910 union of the founding colonies.

The country has six land neighbours: Namibia and Botswana to the north, Zimbabwe to the north-east, Mozambique to the north-east and east, Eswatini to the east, and Lesotho, entirely enclosed within South African territory. Lesotho and Eswatini are the two small pieces of the puzzle, and they come up often in border quizzes.

The flag, adopted in 1994, has six colours, which is rare: black, yellow, green, white, red and blue, joined by a Y-shaped band lying on its side. No other national flag resembles it, which makes it one of the easiest to identify, provided you remember it exists.
