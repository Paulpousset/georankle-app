Guatemala

The most populous country in Central America, whose capital shares its name.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Guatemala has 18.4 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 69th in the world, on 108,890 km², the 106th territory on the list. With about 169 people per square kilometre, it is a dense country, and the most populous of the seven the game files under Central America.

The capital is Guatemala, called Guatemala City to tell it from the country: it is the answer the quiz expects, and one of the cases where the capital’s name and the country’s coincide. The country has four neighbours: Mexico to the north and west, Belize to the north-east, Honduras and El Salvador to the south-east.

It is one of the few countries to touch both the Pacific Ocean, in the south-west, and the Caribbean Sea, in the north-east, through a short front. Its geography is dominated by a chain of volcanoes along the Pacific, several still active, and by the Petén forest, in the north, which extends into Mexico.

The flag has three vertical bands, blue, white, blue, with an emblem at the centre that includes a quetzal, the national bird. It recalls other Central American flags with blue and white bands, such as El Salvador’s or Honduras’s; the quetzal and the coat of arms are the way to avoid a mistake.
