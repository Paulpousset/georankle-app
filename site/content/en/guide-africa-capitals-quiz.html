Africa capitals quiz: 54 capitals, five blocks and nine twin names

The hard part is not memorising fifty-four names, it is not swapping the countries that sound alike. Here is the split and the pairs that trap people, drawn from the game data.

Guide · game data, October 2026

The continent has {{count:afrique}} countries in the game, so {{count:afrique}} capitals to know: more than a quarter of all the questions in a world quiz. It is the first place players lose points, and rarely for the reason you would guess. The relocated capitals (Abuja, Dodoma, Yamoussoukro) are already covered in the list of African capitals; this page deals with the other half of the problem: how to organise the learning, and which confusions to avoid.

Five blocks of six to sixteen countries

The “subregion” field in the game data splits the countries into five very unequal blocks. In each cell the capitals are ordered from the most to the least populous country, so you start with the ones you already know.

{{table:capital-blocks:afrique}}

The two big blocks, Western and Eastern Africa, hold sixteen countries each: split them over two sessions. The smallest, Southern Africa, fits in six names and takes five minutes. The game files Sudan under Northern Africa with Egypt, Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia and Libya: that block is the easiest, since most of its capitals are already familiar.

Nine countries, four names

The game has four families of countries with similar names, nine countries for four confusions. That is where the errors concentrate, because the player knows the name of the country but not which of the two is which.

{{table:capital-set:africa-namesakes}}
  • The three Guineas: Guinea (Conakry), Guinea-Bissau (Bissau) and Equatorial Guinea. The game uses Ciudad de la Paz, the new city on the mainland; Malabo, on the island of Bioko, is the name found in older sources. A tip: each Guinea keeps the language of its former colonial power, French for Guinea, Portuguese for Guinea-Bissau (whose name includes that of its capital) and Spanish for Equatorial Guinea.
  • The two Congos: DR Congo (Kinshasa), 109.3 million people, and the Republic of the Congo (Brazzaville), 6.3 million. The first has more than seventeen times the population of the second. The two capitals face each other across the Congo River.
  • The two Sudans: Sudan (Khartoum) and South Sudan (Juba), separate since 2011.
  • Niger and Nigeria: Niamey for the first, Abuja for the second. Nigeria, with 232.7 million people, has nearly nine times the population of Niger (27.0 million).

Capitals that repeat the country’s name

Five African capitals reuse the name of their country in whole or in part: Djibouti, Tunis (Tunisia), Algiers (Algeria), São Tomé (São Tomé and Príncipe) and Bissau (Guinea-Bissau). They are free answers once you think of them. Their flaw is that a question that easy makes people suspicious, and they look for another answer.

The islands

Six African countries are islands or archipelagos: Madagascar (Antananarivo), Mauritius (Port Louis), the Seychelles (Victoria), the Comoros (Moroni), Cape Verde (Praia) and São Tomé and Príncipe. They are best revised as one block, since their names are all distinct from the mainland’s. The capital of the Seychelles carries the name of Queen Victoria, which many players associate with other cities of the same name.

A three-session plan

  1. Session 1: the obvious ones. Northern and Southern Africa and the capitals that repeat their country’s name. About fifteen capitals in fifteen minutes.
  2. Session 2: East and Middle Africa. Twenty-six countries, to split in two, with the two Congos and the two Sudans handled separately.
  3. Session 3: West Africa and the three Guineas. Sixteen countries, including Niger and Nigeria. Finish with a full round of all {{count:afrique}} countries.

Space the sessions two or three days apart and replay the first the day after the third, as the study guide explains. A missed country is a country replayed in the same session.

Frequently asked questions

How many capitals does Africa have?
{{count:afrique}} in the game, one per country. South Africa has three in practice (Pretoria, Cape Town, Bloemfontein), but the game uses only Pretoria.
What is the capital of the Congo?
It depends which Congo: Brazzaville for the Republic of the Congo, Kinshasa for the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The two cities face each other across the river.
What is the capital of Equatorial Guinea?
The game uses Ciudad de la Paz, a new city built on the mainland. Malabo, on the island of Bioko, is the historic capital and is still widely cited.
How do I avoid confusing Niger and Nigeria?
Niger (Niamey) is a landlocked Sahel country and a former French colony; Nigeria (Abuja) sits on the Gulf of Guinea and has nearly nine times as many people.
