Sudan

Seven neighbours, a twin born in 2011 and a territory that used to be the largest in Africa.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Sudan has 50.4 million people according to the game, thirtieth in the world, but its 1,878,000 km² rank fifteenth by area. The gap between the two ranks signals a large and thinly populated country: 27 people per square kilometre, with the population concentrated along the Nile.

The capital is Khartoum, at the junction of the White Nile and the Blue Nile. No capital trap, but a name trap: Sudan and South Sudan have been two separate countries since the latter’s independence in 2011. South Sudan has Juba as its capital. Before the split, Sudan was the largest country in Africa; the title passed to Algeria.

The game lists seven land neighbours: Libya and Egypt to the north, Eritrea and Ethiopia to the east, South Sudan to the south, the Central African Republic and Chad to the west. Seven is a lot, which makes it a good borders question. Sudan also has a coast on the Red Sea.

The flag has three horizontal bands of red, white and black with a green triangle at the hoist: the pan-Arab colours. It resembles those of several Arab countries, such as Egypt, Syria, Iraq or Yemen, which share red, white and black bands. The green triangle identifies Sudan.
