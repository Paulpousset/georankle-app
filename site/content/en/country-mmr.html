Myanmar (Burma)

Two names, a capital built from scratch and five neighbours including the two most populous countries.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Myanmar has 54.5 million people according to the game, twenty-seventh in the world, on 676,590 km², thirty-ninth by area. The density of 81 people per square kilometre is fairly moderate for the region.

The country has two names. In 1989 the military authorities replaced “Burma” by “Myanmar” in official use; in English both coexist, and the game shows “Myanmar”. Knowing that the two mean the same country avoids losing a question: see also the countries that changed name.

The capital is Naypyidaw, not Yangon. The seat of government moved there in the mid-2000s, to a city built in the middle of the country, far from the coast. Yangon, formerly Rangoon, remains the largest city and the main port. It is one of the newest capitals in the world, and one that players miss most often.

Myanmar has five land neighbours: Bangladesh and India to the west, China to the north-east, Laos and Thailand to the east. It sits between two giants, India and China, the top two countries in the game’s population ranking. Its flag, adopted in 2010, has three bands of yellow, green and red and a large white five-pointed star at the centre.
