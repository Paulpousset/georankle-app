Brazil

Nine neighbours among the eleven other South American countries, and a capital built in four years.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

Brazil is by far the largest country in South America and the fifth in the world by area, with over 8.5 million square kilometres. It is also the seventh country in the world by population: 212 million people according to the game, the great majority living near the coast. The density, around 25 people per square kilometre, stays low: the Amazon covers a good part of the territory.

The capital is Brasília, neither Rio de Janeiro, capital until 1960, nor São Paulo, the biggest city. Brasília was built in a little over three years, between 1956 and 1960, in the very centre of the country, to draw activity away from the coast. It was designed by the urban planner Lúcio Costa and the architect Oscar Niemeyer, and from the air it is recognisable by its plan shaped like an aeroplane.

Brazil touches nine playable neighbours: Colombia, Venezuela, Guyana, Suriname, Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay, Peru and Uruguay. French Guiana, a French department, is a tenth border in reality but is not in the game. Only two South American countries do not touch Brazil: Chile and Ecuador, a very good memory exercise for Borders mode.

Finally, it is the only large South American country where Portuguese is spoken. That feature, inherited from colonisation, is easy to remember and answers many questions.
