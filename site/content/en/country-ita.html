Italy

Two microstates enclosed in its territory, six neighbours in all, and a tricolour mistaken for Ireland’s.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Italy has 59.0 million people according to the game, twenty-fifth in the world. Its 302,070 km² rank seventy-first by area. The density of 195 people per square kilometre is one of the highest among Europe’s large countries, a little over a third of South Korea’s.

The capital is Rome, which is also the largest city. But the capital itself contains another country: Vatican City is enclosed within the city of Rome, and San Marino, further north-east, is surrounded by Italian territory. Italy is therefore the only country in the game to shelter two microstates within its borders.

The game lists six land neighbours: France to the west, Switzerland and Austria to the north, Slovenia to the north-east, plus San Marino and the Vatican. In a borders quiz the last two are the answers people forget, though they count as much as the other four.

The flag is a vertical tricolour of green, white and red. It has two lookalikes: Ireland, the same shape but green, white and orange; and Hungary, red, white and green, but in horizontal bands. For Italy the green is at the hoist. A tip: Italy’s red is a true red, Ireland’s is orange.
