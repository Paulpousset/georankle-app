How it works

Every mode in the game plays multiplayer. What changes is the format.

The duel

One against one, in real time. Both players get exactly the same questions, each on their own side, and the higher score takes the round. You invite a friend by link, or let the game find an opponent. If nobody turns up, a bot steps in so the game starts anyway.

Matches for up to eight

The same idea, extended: up to eight players in one room, on any mode. Everyone answers on their own side, and the standings update round after round.

The custom game

Instead of a single mode, you compose your own sequence: three rounds of flags, two of silhouettes, one of borders. The game runs the programme you chose. It is the format closest to a pub quiz.

The league

For groups that play regularly, a private league offers three challenges a day, identical for all its members, with daily, monthly and all-time standings. You create one in seconds and invite by link.

Local mode

No connection and no account: one phone, passed between players. Everyone plays their turn, the best total wins. It is the format for a car journey or a table.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account to play online?
Yes for duels, matches and leagues — you have to be identifiable between rounds. Local mode and the daily challenge play without one.
What if nobody joins?
A bot steps in after a short delay so the game starts anyway. Its level adapts to yours.
Can several people play on one phone?
Yes, that is local mode: each player takes a turn on the same device, with no connection and no account.
What is a league?
A private group with three challenges a day, identical for all its members, and daily, monthly and all-time standings. You invite by link.
