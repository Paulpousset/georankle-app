Venezuela

Both Caribbean and South American, with three neighbours and a flag that is easily mistaken for Colombia’s and Ecuador’s.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Venezuela has 28.4 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 53rd in the world, over 912,050 km², the 32nd largest territory on the planet. The density of about 31 people per square kilometre hides sharp differences between the heavily populated Caribbean coast and the interior.

The capital is Caracas, in the north, a short distance from the coast. The country opens onto the Caribbean Sea to the north and the Atlantic to the north-east, with the Orinoco delta, one of the great rivers of South America.

Venezuela has three land neighbours: Colombia to the west, Brazil to the south and Guyana to the east. The last is the subject of a long-standing dispute over the Essequibo region, which makes up a large part of Guyanese territory; the game does not rule on the question and keeps to the recognised borders. The country is also home to Angel Falls, the tallest waterfall in the world.

For the flag, the trap is a classic: three horizontal bands of yellow, blue and red, like Colombia and Ecuador, inherited from Gran Colombia. Venezuela is recognised by the arc of white stars on the blue band; Colombia has none, and Ecuador carries its coat of arms at the centre. See the guide to similar flags.
