Countries by population: the top 30

Thirty countries hold more than three quarters of the people in the game. Here is who they are, what each one weighs, and the ranks almost everyone gets wrong.

Guide · game data, October 2026

Ranking countries by population looks simple: take a list, sort it. But the result depends on a prior question, which is the list. The game covers {{countries}} countries, the list explained in our guide to how many countries there are, and every figure on this page refers to them. Together they have 8.10 billion inhabitants. The shares shown below are therefore shares of that total, computed from the game's data rather than copied from somewhere else.

The “Cumulative” column is the most telling: it shows how quickly you reach most of humanity as you go down the list. Continents follow the game's split, which places Russia in Europe.

An extreme concentration

India (1.45 billion) and China (1.41 billion) together weigh 35.3% of the total, more than a third. Add the United States, Indonesia and Pakistan and five countries already reach 46.1%. The top ten pass 57%, the top twenty 69%, the top fifty 86.9%. The last 95 countries on the list, ranks 101 to {{countries}}, add up to only 3.1% of the population.

The gap between the two ends is dizzying. Sixteen countries have more than 100 million inhabitants. At the other end, thirty-eight have fewer than a million, twelve have fewer than a hundred thousand, and the smallest, Vatican City, has 882. The median is modest by comparison: the 98th country, Belarus, has 9.1 million inhabitants, under 1% of India's population.

By continent: Asia dwarfs the rest

Of the game's {{countries}} countries, 47 are in Asia and they hold 58.8% of the population. Africa, with 54 countries, has 18.7%, Europe 9.2% (45 countries), North America 7.5% (23 countries), South America 5.4% (12 countries) and Oceania 0.6% (14 countries). The contrast is striking: Oceania has 14 countries, nearly as many as North America, yet it weighs less than one hundredth of the population.

In the top 30, Asia places thirteen countries, Africa eight, Europe five, North and South America two each, and Oceania none. Its first country, Australia, only comes 54th.

The ranks that trip people up

Five rankings surprise players almost every time:

  • Ethiopia is tenth (132 million), ahead of Mexico (130.9) and Japan (124.0). Nigeria is sixth with 232.7 million, so Africa has two countries in the top ten.
  • Russia is only ninth, with 143.7 million. Being the largest country in the world suggests a matching population, but its density is eight people per square kilometre.
  • Egypt (13th) edges out the Philippines (14th) by under 700,000 people, and DR Congo (15th) passes Vietnam (16th), the opposite of what many expect.
  • The United Kingdom (21st) is ahead of France (23rd) by under 730,000 people, with Tanzania wedged between them at 22nd.
  • Germany, 19th with 83.5 million, is the second most populous country in Europe after Russia.

Remembering the ranking in tiers

Learning thirty ranks in a row is pointless. The ranking breaks into tiers of similar populations, inside which the exact order rarely matters in practice:

  1. The two giants: India, then China, above 1.4 billion.
  2. Between 200 and 350 million: United States, Indonesia, Pakistan, Nigeria, Brazil.
  3. Between 100 and 175 million: Bangladesh, Russia, Ethiopia, Mexico, Japan, Egypt, the Philippines, DR Congo, Vietnam.
  4. Between 50 and 100 million: from Iran to Uganda, including the large European countries (Germany, the United Kingdom, France, Italy) and South Korea.

This block method is the one developed in the revision guide: an approximate global order rather than an exact list. It is enough to settle most comparisons between two countries.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the most populous country in the world?
According to the game's data, India, with 1,450,935,791 inhabitants, ahead of China (1,408,975,000). Together they represent 35.3% of the population of the game's {{countries}} countries.
How many countries have more than 100 million people?
Sixteen, according to the game's data, from India down to Vietnam. The sixteenth, Vietnam, has just over 101 million inhabitants.
Which is the least populous country?
Vatican City, with 882 inhabitants. Next come Tuvalu (9,646), Nauru (11,947) and Palau (17,695).
Why do the figures differ from one website to another?
Because population is an estimate that depends on the reference year and on the list of countries used. The figures on this page are the game's own; the order of the big countries is stable, but close ranks (the United Kingdom and France, for instance) can flip depending on the source.
