Guinea

Three countries in the game are called Guinea. This one, the largest and most populous, has its capital at Conakry and six neighbours, which makes it a crossroads of West Africa.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

The game data gives Guinea 14.8 million inhabitants, 74th in the world and 27th in Africa, on 245,860 km², the 77th territory by size. Density, around 60 people per square kilometre, remains moderate for the region.

The capital is Conakry, built at the tip of a peninsula jutting into the Atlantic. The name also serves to tell the country apart in conversation: people say “Guinea-Conakry” to avoid confusing it with Guinea-Bissau, its north-western neighbour, or with Equatorial Guinea, in Central Africa. Papua New Guinea, in Oceania, completes the set of namesakes, a trap covered in the Africa capitals quiz.

Guinea has six neighbours, which puts it among the fifteen countries in the game with exactly that number: Guinea-Bissau, Senegal and Mali to the north, Ivory Coast to the east, Liberia and Sierra Leone to the south. Its interior is dominated by the Fouta Djallon highlands, often called the water tower of West Africa: the Niger, the Senegal and the Gambia rivers all rise there before flowing into other countries.

The flag is a vertical tricolour of red, yellow and green. Mali carries exactly the same colours in reverse order, green, yellow, red: look at the band next to the pole, red for Guinea, green for Mali.
