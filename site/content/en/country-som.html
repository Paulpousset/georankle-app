Somalia

The Horn of Africa, three neighbours and an immense coastline on the Indian Ocean.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Somalia has 19.0 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 67th in the world, on 637,660 km², the 42nd territory on the list. Density is about 30 people per square kilometre; a large share of the population is pastoral and moves with the herds, which makes the average not very telling.

The capital is Mogadishu, on the Indian Ocean coast. Somalia has three neighbours in the game: Djibouti to the north-west, Ethiopia to the west, Kenya to the south-west. The game does not count Somaliland, which declared itself independent but is not recognised: see the guide to disputed territories.

On the globe, you recognise it by its horn shape: the country draws the eastern tip of Africa, and its coastline, running from the Gulf of Aden to the Kenyan border, is the longest of the continental mainland. It is a good landmark for locating the Arabian Sea, the Arabian Peninsula opposite, and the Bab-el-Mandeb strait.

The flag is the simplest in the region: a sky-blue field with a white five-pointed star. In a flag quiz, it is told apart from other star flags by carrying no other figure.
