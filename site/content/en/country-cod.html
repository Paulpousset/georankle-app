DR Congo

Eleventh in the world by area, nine neighbours, and two Congos that must not be confused.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

The Democratic Republic of the Congo, or DR Congo, has 109 million people according to the game, fifteenth in the world. It is also the eleventh largest country by area, at 2.34 million km², just behind Algeria for the title of largest in Africa. That size shows in a low density, 47 people per square kilometre.

The capital is Kinshasa, the country’s largest city, on the Congo River. Across the river lies Brazzaville, capital of the Republic of the Congo: two national capitals facing each other a few kilometres apart, a rare case. That is the country’s number one trap: there are two Congos in the game, and the capital tells them apart. The country was called Zaire from 1971 to 1997.

DR Congo has nine land neighbours, as many as Brazil and Germany: the Congo to the west, the Central African Republic and South Sudan to the north, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi and Tanzania to the east, Zambia and Angola to the south. Its outlet to the Atlantic, only a few dozen kilometres wide, is among the narrowest in the world.

The current flag is sky blue, crossed by a red diagonal edged in yellow, with a yellow star in the corner.
