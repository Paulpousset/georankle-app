South Korea

One neighbour, a narrow peninsula and the second highest density in the population top 30, behind Bangladesh.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

South Korea has 51.8 million people according to the game, twenty-ninth in the world, on only 100,450 km², which ranks it one hundred and eighth by area. The result is a density of 515 people per square kilometre, the second highest among the thirty most populous countries, behind Bangladesh alone.

The capital is Seoul, which is also the largest city. The trap is a swap with the North: Pyongyang is the capital of North Korea, Seoul that of the South. A simple way to remember: the southern capital is the one whose name keeps coming up in economic and cultural news.

The game lists a single land neighbour: North Korea, to the north, along a border crossed by the demilitarised zone. Everything else is maritime, between the Yellow Sea, the Sea of Japan and the Korea Strait. In the population top 30, only South Korea and the United Kingdom have exactly one neighbour; all the others have none or several.

The flag, the Taegeukgi, is a white field with a red and blue circle at the centre, surrounded by four black trigrams in the corners. It resembles few other flags, but players mostly remember the trigrams. The northern flag is quite different: blue, white and red bands with a white star in a red disc.
