What GeoRankle was

In spring 2026 the game was called GeoRankle and did exactly one thing: Rankle. Eight countries shown one at a time, eight ranking criteria — population, area, coastline length, passport power… — and one slot per criterion. For each country you had to pick the criterion where it ranks best in the world, then compare your total to the optimal placement.

The name said it all: “Geo” for geography, “Rankle” for ranks. It was a ranking game, not a map game — you could shine at it without being able to place Turkmenistan.

What changed

The game took the name GeoG when other modes grew around Rankle: flags, capitals, the 3D globe, country shapes, borders… {{modes}} modes today, all covering the {{countries}} UN member states, with a daily challenge shared by every player, online duels, a ranked mode and a 300-level story mode.

The site and the app now speak sixteen languages, and the game runs on iPhone, Android and in the browser, with a single account.

What did not change

  • The Rankle rule: eight countries, eight criteria, each criterion used once, and a score given as an efficiency percentage against the optimal placement. The full rules have their own page.
  • The {{themes}} criteria, drawn from public sources (World Bank, UN, UNESCO, sports federations), listed on the mode page.
  • The Streak variant — one country, four criteria, one mistake ends the run — which has its page too.
  • Free to play: no account for solo play, nothing to buy to reach any mode.

Where to play GeoRankle today

Three doors, one rule:

  • Every day, in the daily Rankle: the same grid for everyone, a single attempt, a score to compare.

Frequently asked

Are GeoRankle and Rankle the same thing?
Almost. GeoRankle was the name of the whole game when it had a single mode; Rankle is the name of that mode, which still exists in GeoG with the same rule.
Where is the GeoRankle app?
It is called GeoG. On the App Store and Google Play, search for “GeoG” or follow the links on this page: Rankle is in the menu with the other modes.
Do I need an account to play Rankle?
No. Rankle, Streak and every solo mode start without one. The free account is for duels, ranked play and the leaderboards.
Is the game still free?
Yes. There is nothing to buy to play Rankle or any of the {{modes}} modes.
