Ecuador

A country named after the line that crosses it, and a capital perched in the Andes.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Ecuador has 18.1 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 70th in the world, on 256,370 km², the 76th territory on the list. Density is about 71 people per square kilometre, in a small country where the Andes, the coast and the Amazon share the territory.

The capital is Quito, nestled in the Andes at nearly 2,800 metres, not Guayaquil, the largest city and the great port of the coast, which people type by reflex. Ecuador has just two neighbours, Colombia to the north and Peru to the south and east, which makes it one of the simplest countries on the map for the Borders mode.

Its name comes from the equator, which crosses it in the north, near Quito. The country also includes the Galápagos Islands, in the Pacific, about 1,000 km off the coast, famous for their wildlife and for having inspired Darwin; the game does not count them separately.

The flag has three horizontal bands, yellow (double width), blue and red, with a coat of arms at the centre. It is the same order as Colombia’s and Venezuela’s, heirs of Gran Colombia: only the coat of arms, or Venezuela’s stars, tells them apart.
