South Sudan

Born in 2011 from a separation from Sudan, it is the youngest of the game’s {{countries}} countries, and one of the least known for its size.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

South Sudan has 11.9 million inhabitants according to the game data, 80th in the world and 32nd in Africa. Its territory is vast: 646,883 km², the 41st in the world and the 18th in Africa, more than metropolitan France. Density, about 18 people per square kilometre, is among the lowest on the continent outside the Sahara.

The capital is Juba, on the White Nile, in the far south of the country, near the Ugandan border. The river then crosses the Sudd, one of the largest marshlands in the world, which occupies the centre of the territory and makes it hard to travel through for much of the year.

The country is landlocked, with six neighbours: Sudan to the north, Ethiopia to the east, Kenya and Uganda to the south-east and south, the Democratic Republic of the Congo to the south-west and the Central African Republic to the west. It is one of the fifteen countries in the game with exactly six neighbours. It gained independence on 9 July 2011, after a referendum, which makes it the last state admitted to the UN and therefore the most recent in the game, a case mentioned in the guide on how many countries there are in the world.

The flag takes up the black, red and green stripes separated by white fimbriations, like Kenya’s, and adds a blue triangle at the hoist bearing a yellow star. Do not confuse it with the flag of Sudan, red, white and black with a green triangle.
