Australia

A continent-country with no land neighbour, and a capital that is neither Sydney nor Melbourne.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Australia has 27.2 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 54th, but its 7,741,220 km² make it the sixth largest country in the world. Density falls to about 3.5 people per square kilometre, one of the lowest on the planet: the population is concentrated on the coasts, mostly in the south-east.

The capital is Canberra, a purpose-built city in the south-eastern interior, midway between Sydney and Melbourne. The two big cities were rivals for the title, and the capital was founded between them: it is the usual quiz trap, since the reflex answer is Sydney.

Australia has no land neighbour in the game: it occupies an entire continent alone, surrounded by the Indian and Pacific oceans. Its nearest neighbours are islands or island nations, such as New Zealand to the east, Papua New Guinea and Indonesia to the north. The game files it under Oceania.

Its flag carries the Union Jack in the corner, a large seven-pointed star beneath it and the Southern Cross on the right. It closely resembles New Zealand’s, whose stars are red, smaller and four in number.
