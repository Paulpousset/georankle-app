France

Eight neighbours, three of them tiny, and a count that changes depending on whether overseas land is included.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

France has 68.6 million people according to the game, twenty-third in the world, and 606,410 km², forty-fifth by area. The density is 113 people per square kilometre. These figures are those of mainland France: the game does not count the overseas departments, which changes the number of neighbours.

The capital is Paris. Nothing to report, except that it is one of the few capitals whose exact spot on the map almost everyone knows, which makes it a good anchor for placing the rest of Western Europe.

The game lists eight land neighbours: Belgium, Luxembourg and Germany in the north-east, Switzerland and Italy in the east, Monaco on the Mediterranean coast, Spain and Andorra in the south. Three of them are microstates, so France is a good drill for learning Monaco and Andorra without searching the whole map. If French Guiana were counted, Suriname and Brazil would join the list; the game, which keeps only the mainland, does not count them.

The flag is a vertical tricolour of blue, white and red. Tricolours are common in Europe and two confusions come up: the Netherlands has the same colours, but in horizontal bands and in the order red, white, blue; Italy also has three vertical bands, but green, white, red. For France, the blue is at the hoist.
