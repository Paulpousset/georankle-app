Kazakhstan

A giant of over 2.7 million km², the largest landlocked country in the world, and almost empty.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Kazakhstan has 20.6 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 63rd in the world, but it covers 2,724,902 km², the ninth largest area on the list. The result is a density of about 8 people per square kilometre: one of the biggest demographic voids in the world, steppe and semi-desert as far as the eye can see.

The capital is Astana, which replaced Almaty in 1997; the city was named Nur-Sultan from 2019 to 2022 (see the guide to countries that changed name). Almaty, in the south-east, remains the largest city, the reflex answer to avoid in a quiz.

The game counts five neighbours: Russia to the north and west, China to the east, and three Central Asian countries to the south, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Turkmenistan. It has no access to the ocean, even though it borders the Caspian Sea, which is a closed lake; in the game’s data it is filed as having no coastline.

The flag is sky blue, decorated with a golden sun and a steppe eagle, with a vertical ornamental pattern on the hoist side. Sky blue is rare among national flags, which makes it easy to spot.
