Countries by area: the top 30

Six countries cover nearly half of the game's total land area. Here is the top 30, its surprises, and the reasons a flat map distorts our sense of size.

Guide · game data, October 2026

Ask anyone for the largest country in the world and they will get it right: Russia. Ask for the tenth and the answers scatter. Area is nevertheless one of the most stable facts in geography, far more than population. The figures on this page are the game's own for its {{countries}} countries, whose combined area is 140.1 million km². The shares shown are shares of that total, computed from the game's data.

Continents follow the game's split, which places Russia in Europe. That is why the first country on the list is labelled “Europe” although it also stretches across Asia.

A predictable podium, a real concentration

Russia (17.1 million km²) alone weighs 12.2% of the total, which is 5.2 times India, seventh on the list, and more than 28 times the game's metropolitan France (606,410 km²). Canada follows with 11.2%, then the United States (7.0%), China (6.8%), Brazil (6.1%) and Australia (5.5%). Those six countries add up to 48.8% of the area. The top ten reach 56.8%, the top twenty 69.6%.

The most startling figure sits at the other end: Russia is roughly 35 million times larger than Vatican City, which covers only 0.49 km². The median country, the 98th, is Eritrea, at 121,766 km²: a typical country is much closer to Greece than to Brazil.

United States and China: a ranking that depends on the source

In the game's data the United States (9,831,510 km²) is ahead of China (9,562,910 km²) by about 269,000 km², under 3%. The gap is small, and the order of these two countries changes depending on the source and on how inland waters are counted. This is not a mistake: it shows that beyond a few giants, an area is a convention as much as a measurement. The same holds a notch lower for Algeria (2,381,740 km²) and DR Congo (2,344,860 km²), only 37,000 km² apart.

The surprises

  • Kazakhstan is ninth (2.72 million km²), ahead of Algeria and Saudi Arabia. It is the largest landlocked country in the game, and the only landlocked country in the top ten.
  • Algeria is the largest country in Africa, tenth in the world, with DR Congo right behind in eleventh. Sudan (15th) and Libya (16th) complete a group of very large African countries.
  • India is only seventh, just behind Australia, although its population puts it first. By far the densest of the top seven: 441 people per km², against 147 for China and 8 for Russia.
  • Mongolia (18th) is larger than Peru, Chad or Angola, but its density is only 2.3 people per km², the lowest in the game.
  • Seven of the thirty largest countries have no coastline: Kazakhstan, Mongolia, Chad, Niger, Mali, Ethiopia and Bolivia.

By continent, who dominates?

Thirteen of the thirty largest countries are African, a reminder that Africa is not a small continent: with its 54 countries it accounts for 21.5% of the game's area, nearly as much as Russia and Canada combined (23.4%). Asia has seven in the top 30, South America five, North America three, and Europe and Oceania one each. By continent, the split of area is far more even than the split of population: Asia weighs 22.8%, Africa 21.5%, North America 20.2%, Europe 16.8%, South America 12.6% and Oceania 6.1%.

Why the map fools us

Most maps shown on screens use the Mercator projection, which stretches surfaces the further you move from the equator. Countries near the poles, such as Russia, Canada or the Nordic countries, look much bigger than they are, and equatorial countries look smaller. The table above gives the true proportions. This is also the point of the Silhouettes mode: without the map's setting, a shape alone does not tell you its size, and you find out how hard that is to judge.

To remember this ranking, one simple way is to memorise the top six (Russia, Canada, the United States, China, Brazil, Australia), then the large “desert” countries: Kazakhstan, Algeria, Saudi Arabia, Libya and Sudan, which sit between ranks 9 and 16.

Vatican City, at 0.49 km². Nauru (20 km²) and Tuvalu (30 km²) follow. The full list of microstates is in our guide to them.
Which is the largest country in Africa?
Algeria, with 2,381,740 km², tenth in the world. DR Congo (2,344,860 km²) is very close behind.
Which is the largest landlocked country?
Kazakhstan, with 2,724,902 km², ninth in the world ranking. It is the only landlocked country in the top ten.
