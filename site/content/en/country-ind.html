India

The most populous country in the world, with a capital people readily confuse with its best-known city.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

In the game's data, India is the most populous country on the planet: 1.45 billion people, ahead of China. The switch is recent, and it is a good example of what a quiz has to keep up to date: for decades the answer to “which country has the most people?” was China. The game simply follows its data.

The capital is New Delhi. It is neither Mumbai, the city that cinema and finance made famous, nor Calcutta, capital of British India until 1911. That year the British moved power to Delhi, and the administrative district of New Delhi was inaugurated in 1931. In a quiz the expected answer is New Delhi, never Delhi alone or Mumbai.

On the map India is easy to place, but its neighbourhood is harder. The game counts six playable land neighbours: Pakistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, Myanmar and Nepal. Nearby Sri Lanka is an island and so does not appear. Bangladesh is a textbook case for Borders mode: it is almost entirely wrapped by India, which leaves it a single other border, with Myanmar.

Finally, India is a country of contrasts: seventh in the world by area, yet with a density above four hundred people per square kilometre, more than twelve times that of the United States. That is why so many “higher or lower” questions on population involve it.
