Pakistan

A purpose-built capital, the world's fifth-largest population and four neighbours.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

With 251 million people according to the game's data, Pakistan is the fifth country in the world by population, just behind Indonesia. Its area, under 800,000 km², puts it only thirty-fifth: at about 316 people per square kilometre, it is a far denser country than China.

The capital is Islamabad, not Karachi, the largest city and former capital, nor Lahore. Islamabad was built from the late 1950s to replace Karachi, judged too far off-centre on the coast; it is a planned city, like Brasília or Canberra. In the Capitals quiz it is a classic trap, since few players know the city that came before it.

Pakistan has four land neighbours: Iran to the west, Afghanistan to the north-west, China to the north-east and India to the east. Its border with India, drawn in 1947 at the partition of British India, is one of the most closely watched in the world. To the south it opens onto the Arabian Sea.

Finally, its name is said to be an acronym, coined in the 1930s from the regions of north-western British India. Remember above all that “pak” means “pure” in Urdu and that the country is the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.
