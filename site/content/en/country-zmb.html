Zambia

Eight neighbours, no coast, and a river doing duty as its southern frontier.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Zambia has 21.3 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 62nd in the world, on 752,610 km², the 38th territory on the list. Density is only about 28 people per square kilometre: a large, thinly populated country, with a good share of its people living around the capital and the Copperbelt, in the north.

The capital is Lusaka, in the south of the country. Zambia is landlocked and has eight neighbours, as many as Austria or Türkiye in the game: DR Congo to the north, Tanzania to the north-east, Malawi to the east, Mozambique, Zimbabwe, Botswana and Namibia to the south, and Angola to the west. It is a true crossroads of southern Africa.

The Zambezi, which gave the country its name, draws the border with Zimbabwe, along which lie Victoria Falls. Further west, the border with Botswana is one of the shortest in the world, on the river. To remember the shape: Zambia looks like a butterfly with its wings spread.

The flag is green, with an eagle in flight and three vertical bands, red, black and orange, in the lower fly corner. Few flags carry an animal: this one is easy to recognise once spotted.
