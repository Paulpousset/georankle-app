The capitals of Oceania

Fourteen countries, eleven of them tiny island states. It is the smallest continent in the game, and by far the least known.

Reference · {{count:oceanie}} countries · game data, updated August 2026

Oceania has only {{count:oceanie}} countries — three times fewer than Europe, four times fewer than Africa. It is also the least known: outside Australia and New Zealand, almost nobody can name a single capital. Twelve names are enough to cover everything else.

The two famous traps

Canberra, not Sydney. The choice dates from 1908 and answers the same problem as Ottawa or Washington: Sydney and Melbourne were fighting over the title, so a new city was built exactly between them. Wellington, not Auckland: New Zealand moved its capital to the Cook Strait in 1865, to get closer to the centre of the country and to the South Island, which was threatening to secede.

The atoll capitals

The rest of Oceania sits on islands whose total area does not exceed that of a small county. Nauru, 21 km², officially has no capital: Yaren, where parliament sits, is the name kept by convention. Palau moved its seat in 2006 to Ngerulmud, a capital of barely a few hundred inhabitants, the least populous in the world. Tuvalu has Funafuti, an atoll whose runway doubles as a playing field between flights.

These tiny countries weigh heavily in a quiz: in a random draw, Tuvalu has exactly the same chance of appearing as Australia. It is also why some GeoG modes exclude Oceania from their most demanding variants — the pool simply is not wide enough.

Frequently asked

What is the capital of Australia?
Canberra, chosen in 1908 and built between Sydney and Melbourne, which were fighting over the title. Sydney remains the largest city.
What is the smallest capital in the world?
Ngerulmud, in Palau: a few hundred inhabitants. The country moved its seat there in 2006.
Does Nauru have a capital?
Officially no. Yaren, where parliament sits, is the name kept by convention in atlases and in GeoG.
