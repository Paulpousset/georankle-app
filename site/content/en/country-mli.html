Mali

Seven neighbours, the Niger river and a green, yellow and red flag that other countries carry the other way round.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Mali has 24.5 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 58th in the world, over 1,240,190 km², the 23rd largest territory on the planet. Density is only about 20 people per square kilometre: the north is desert, and people live in the south, along the Niger river.

The capital is Bamako, in the south-west, on the river. Mali is landlocked and has seven neighbours: Algeria to the north, Niger to the east, Burkina Faso and Ivory Coast to the south-east and south, Guinea to the south-west, Senegal and Mauritania to the west.

The country is a central piece of West Africa: it touches both the French-speaking countries of the Sahel and those of the coast. Timbuktu, on the bend of the river, is the city that made it famous, but it is not the capital, which a quiz can exploit.

The flag consists of three vertical bands, green, yellow and red, from hoist to fly. Guinea carries the same colours in reverse order, red, yellow, green; Senegal has Mali’s order with a green star in the centre; and Ghana has them horizontally. A simple trick: in Mali, green touches the hoist.
