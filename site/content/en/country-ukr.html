Ukraine

Seven land borders and a flag that reads from top to bottom.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Ukraine has 37.9 million people according to the game, forty-first in the world, spread over 603,550 km², the forty-sixth largest territory on the planet. The game files it under Eastern Europe with Russia, Belarus and Moldova, and those three neighbours are the easiest way to revise it as a group.

The capital is Kyiv, spelled Kiev in French and in older English sources. The city straddles the Dnieper, the river that structures the whole country. There is no capital trap here: Kyiv is also the largest city, a relief in a quiz where exceptions abound.

What makes Ukraine valuable for a Borders player is the number of neighbours: seven. Belarus and Russia lie to the north, Russia again to the east, Poland, Slovakia, Hungary and Romania to the west, and Moldova to the south-west, which Ukraine wraps around on three sides. To the south the country opens onto the Black Sea. Few European countries sit at the junction of three such different zones: Central Europe, the Balkans through Romania, and the eastern European plain.

The flag is two equal horizontal bands, blue above and yellow below, usually read as sky over a wheat field. The trap is Sweden: the same colours, but a yellow cross on a blue field. If the field is plain and split in two, it is Ukraine; if there is a cross, it is Sweden (see the Nordic flags).
