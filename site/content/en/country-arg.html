Argentina

The largest Spanish-speaking country by area, and second in South America.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Argentina has 45.7 million people according to the game, thirty-fifth in the world, on 2,780,400 km², eighth by area. It has only 16 people per square kilometre, because a large part of its territory is taken up by the pampas, the Andes and Patagonia.

The capital is Buenos Aires, also the largest city. There is no capital trap, but it is often confused with Santiago, the capital of neighbouring Chile, or with Brasília. On the continent, only Brazil is bigger.

The game lists five land neighbours: Chile to the west, along the Andes, Bolivia and Paraguay to the north, Brazil and Uruguay to the east. The border with Chile is one of the longest in the world and follows the crest of the Andes, where Aconcagua, the highest peak of the Americas, rises. Argentina also shares Tierra del Fuego with Chile.

The flag has three horizontal bands, sky blue, white, sky blue, with a yellow sun in the centre of the white band. It is told apart from Uruguay’s by the absence of stripes and a canton at the top.
