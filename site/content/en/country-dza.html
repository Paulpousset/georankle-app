Algeria

The largest country in Africa since Sudan split in 2011.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Algeria has 46.8 million people according to the game, thirty-third in the world, but its 2,381,740 km² rank tenth by area. The density is only 20 people per square kilometre. The average is misleading: the vast Sahara covers most of the south, and the population is concentrated in the north, near the Mediterranean.

The capital is Algiers, on the coast, also the largest city. There is no capital trap, but a map trap: on a blank map the country is often confused with Libya, its eastern neighbour, or with Mali to the south.

The game lists six land neighbours: Morocco to the west, Tunisia and Libya to the east, and three Sahel countries to the south, Mauritania, Mali and Niger. It is a good borders question, because part of the line follows straight lines drawn through the desert. Algeria has been the largest country in Africa since 2011, when Sudan lost South Sudan.

The flag is split into two vertical bands, green and white, with a red crescent and star in the centre. Do not confuse it with Tunisia’s, which is red with a white disc, or Mauritania’s, green with two red bands.
