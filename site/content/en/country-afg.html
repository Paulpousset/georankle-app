Afghanistan

A landlocked country with six neighbours, including a narrow corridor that touches China.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Afghanistan has 42.6 million people according to the game, thirty-sixth in the world, on 652,870 km², fortieth by area. The density, about 65 people per square kilometre, is modest: most of the country is mountainous, crossed by the Hindu Kush.

The capital is Kabul, also the largest city. There is no capital trap, but a regional trap: Afghanistan is often grouped with its Central Asian neighbours, whose “-stan” names are easily confused.

The game lists six land neighbours: Iran to the west, Pakistan to the south and east, and three former Soviet republics to the north, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan and Tajikistan. The sixth is China, which the country touches through the Wakhan Corridor, a very narrow strip of mountain in the far east. The country is landlocked, with no direct access to the sea.

The Afghan flag has been changed many times over the past century, with each change of regime, so a flag quiz is not the best way to learn this country. Remember its position instead, in the centre of Asia, between Iran to the west and Pakistan to the east.
