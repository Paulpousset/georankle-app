Madagascar

An island with no land border at all: that is its whole appeal for a quiz.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Madagascar has 32.0 million people according to the game, forty-ninth in the world. At 587,295 km² it is the forty-eighth largest territory on the planet, and above all one of the biggest islands in the world. Density is about 54 people per square kilometre. The Mozambique Channel cuts it off from mainland Africa, which makes it a case apart in the game’s split, where it is still filed under Africa.

The capital is Antananarivo, in the heart of the highlands, far from the sea. It is also the largest city. The name is long but easy to learn by syllables, and the local short form “Tana” often goes with it. It is among the hardest African capitals to spell, one to watch in the Africa capitals quiz.

The point the game stresses is that there is no land neighbour. In the Borders mode, Madagascar is therefore an impossible answer: no border to name. In the location modes, though, it is easy to find: look for the long island running north to south off Mozambique, in the Indian Ocean. Its sea neighbours are Comoros, Mauritius, Réunion and the Seychelles, much smaller islands not to be mixed up with it.

The flag has a white vertical band at the hoist and two horizontal bands, red above and green below. It is one of the few African flags to combine one vertical and two horizontal bands, which makes it fairly easy to spot. Lemurs, found in the wild only on Madagascar, are the best mnemonic for the island.
