Nigeria

Africa's demographic giant, whose capital is not its best-known city.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

Nigeria is the most populous country in Africa: 233 million people according to the game, which puts it sixth in the world. On the continent, Ethiopia comes second. Its area of under a million square kilometres, though, puts it only thirty-first: the country is markedly denser than its large African neighbours.

The capital is Abuja, not Lagos, the biggest city and economic capital. In the mid-1970s the government decided to build a new capital in the middle of the country, roughly equidistant from the major regional groups, rather than on the coast; the official move dates from 1991. It is the same logic as in Brazil or Pakistan: a brand-new capital conceived as a compromise.

Nigeria has four land neighbours: Benin to the west, Niger to the north, Chad to the north-east and Cameroon to the east. To the south it faces the Gulf of Guinea, on the Atlantic Ocean. Do not confuse it with its neighbour Niger: both names come from the Niger River, which flows through the two countries, and they differ by only a few letters.

On a flag, Nigeria's is one of the easiest to remember: three vertical stripes, green, white, green, with no emblem.
