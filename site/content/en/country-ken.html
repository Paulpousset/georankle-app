Kenya

Crossed by the equator, bordering five countries, and one of three states sharing Lake Victoria.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Kenya has 56.4 million people according to the game, twenty-sixth in the world, on 591,969 km², forty-seventh by area. The density of 95 people per square kilometre hides sharp contrasts: people gather in the south-western highlands and on the coast, while the north is arid.

The capital is Nairobi, also the largest city, and nobody gets caught on it. The trap is regional: Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania form an East African trio whose capitals (Nairobi, Kampala, Dodoma) and outlines are often confused.

The country has five land neighbours: South Sudan and Ethiopia to the north, Somalia to the east, Tanzania to the south, Uganda to the west. Lake Victoria, the largest lake in Africa, is shared by Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, three countries that meet there. The equator crosses Kenya, which makes it easier to place on a globe: it sits in the middle, neither wholly north nor south.

The flag is made of black, red and green bands, separated by white fringes, with a Maasai shield and two crossed spears at the centre. It is one of the most recognisable flags in Africa, because it is one of the few that carries an object at the centre rather than a plain geometric design.
