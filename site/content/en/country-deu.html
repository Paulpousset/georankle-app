Germany

Nine neighbours, Europe’s second-largest population, and a country at the heart of Europe.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Germany has 83.5 million people according to the game: nineteenth in the world and second in Europe behind Russia. It covers 358,000 km² (sixty-third by area), giving a density of 233 people per square kilometre, one of the highest among large European countries.

The capital is Berlin, also the largest city. West Germany’s capital was Bonn from 1949 until reunification in 1990; Berlin became the capital of the whole country again after that date. The local name of the country, Deutschland, is worth knowing for quizzes in the original version.

With nine land neighbours, Germany has more than any European country except Russia (fourteen): Denmark to the north, Poland and Czechia to the east, Austria and Switzerland to the south, France, Luxembourg, Belgium and the Netherlands to the west. It shares the nine-neighbour mark with Brazil and DR Congo. Its central position makes it an excellent anchor for learning Europe step by step.

The flag is a horizontal tricolour of black, red and gold. Belgium’s uses the same colours (black, yellow, red) but in vertical stripes: remember that Germany’s run horizontally.
