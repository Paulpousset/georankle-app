Bolivia

A huge, sparsely populated country perched on the Altiplano, with two capitals in the atlases and a single one in the quiz.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Bolivia has 12.4 million inhabitants according to the game data, 78th in the world and eighth of the twelve countries of South America. Its area, 1,098,580 km², puts it on the other hand at the 27th rank worldwide and fifth on the continent: density does not exceed 11 people per square kilometre, one of the lowest in the Americas.

The game keeps Sucre as the capital, the constitutional capital and seat of the Supreme Court. La Paz houses the government and parliament, and Santa Cruz de la Sierra, in the eastern lowlands, is the most populous city without claiming either title. The case is detailed in the South America capitals quiz.

Bolivia has five neighbours: Brazil to the north and east, Paraguay to the south-east, Argentina to the south, Chile to the south-west and Peru to the west. It is landlocked, and with Paraguay the only landlocked country on the continent. That is not a geographical fate: the country had a Pacific coast until the War of the Pacific, lost to Chile at the end of the nineteenth century, and it still claims access to the sea. Lake Titicaca, shared with Peru, and the Salar de Uyuni are the two landmarks of the Andean west.

The flag is a horizontal tricolour of red, yellow and green, with the coat of arms in the centre in its state version. Ghana uses the same bands with a black star; in South America no other country uses this combination.
