Colombia

Five neighbours, two oceans, and a tricolour whose cousins are Ecuador and Venezuela.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Colombia has 52.9 million people according to the game, twenty-eighth in the world, on 1,140,619 km², twenty-fifth by area. The density of 46 people per square kilometre is fairly low, since most people live in the Andean valleys and highlands rather than in the Amazon forest.

The capital is Bogotá, also the largest city, built on a high plateau in the Andes. The difficulty is in the name: Colombia (with an “o”) must not be confused with Columbia, as in the District of Columbia in Washington, or with British Columbia, a Canadian province. All derive from Christopher Columbus, but they name three places with nothing in common.

The country has five land neighbours: Panama to the north-west, Venezuela to the east, Brazil to the south-east, Peru and Ecuador to the south. It is the only South American country on both the Caribbean Sea and the Pacific Ocean, which makes it a crossing point between Central America and the rest of the continent.

The flag has three horizontal bands, yellow (double width), blue, red. Ecuador and Venezuela have almost identical flags: all three descend from Gran Colombia, the state of the early nineteenth century. To tell them apart, look at the details: Ecuador carries a coat of arms at the centre, Venezuela an arc of stars on the blue band, Colombia nothing at all.
