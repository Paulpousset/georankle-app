Privacy Policy — GeoG

Last updated: September 20, 2026

GeoG ("the app") is a geography game with leaderboards and multiplayer matches. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account: email address and password (encrypted), to create and secure your account.
  • Profile: username, optional profile picture, and gameplay stats (matches, ELO score, rankings).
  • Game data: match results, invitations and multiplayer history.
  • Usage and diagnostics data: usage events (screens viewed, games started, in-app actions) tied to an internal identifier, and error/crash reports. This data does not contain sensitive personal information.

How we use data

Data is used to operate the app (authentication, leaderboards, matchmaking, profile display) and to understand how the app is used and fix bugs, in order to improve it. We do not sell your data, and account and game data are never used to target advertising; what advertising networks collect on their side is described in the “Advertising” section below.

Hosting

Data is stored and processed via Supabase, our database and authentication provider.

Analytics and diagnostics

To understand app usage and improve reliability, we use:

  • PostHog — product analytics (usage events, aggregated statistics), hosted in the European Union.
  • Sentry — error and crash reporting, hosted in the European Union.

These services process an internal identifier and technical data (device type, app version), with no advertising or resale.

PostHog analytics also covers the playgeog.com website (pages viewed, referring page). It stores an anonymous identifier in your browser's local storage and a cookie, solely to count visits; the data is collected through our own domain and stays hosted in the European Union.

Advertising

GeoG is free and funded by advertising. On playgeog.com, ads are served by Google AdSense, only on content pages and on desktop; in the app, by Google AdMob, as videos the player chooses to watch to earn coins or a life. No ads are shown on error pages or on this page.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party advertising cookies at aboutads.info. How Google uses data from partner sites is described at policies.google.com.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised ads are only served with your consent, requested on your first visit and changeable at any time. Without consent, ads remain non-personalised.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the app: Profile → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. You may also contact us at the address below.
