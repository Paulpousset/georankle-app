Mexico

Eleventh in the world by population, thirteenth by area, and just three land borders.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Mexico has 131 million people according to the game, making it the eleventh most populous country in the world and the largest Spanish-speaking one. At 1.96 million km² it ranks thirteenth by area, just ahead of Indonesia. Its density, about 67 people per square kilometre, is moderate: much of the country is arid or mountainous, and people cluster on the central plateau.

The capital is Mexico City, which shares its name with the country, a trap for beginners (the game writes “Mexico City” in English and “Mexico” in French). It is also the country’s largest urban area, built on the former Aztec capital. There is no capital puzzle here: one capital, no rival.

Mexico has only three land neighbours: the United States to the north, Guatemala and Belize to the south-east. The northern border is one of the busiest in the world, and it gives the country a hinge position between North and Central America. On the game’s map it is filed under North America, which sometimes surprises players who group it under “Latin America”.

The flag is a vertical tricolour of green, white and red, with an eagle perched on a cactus in the middle, a snake in its beak. That emblem is what tells it apart from Italy’s flag, which uses the same colours and carries no figure at all.
