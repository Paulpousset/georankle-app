Cameroon

Yaoundé is the capital, Douala the big port city, and the country has two official languages.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

With 29.1 million inhabitants, Cameroon is the 52nd most populous country in the game, and its 475,440 km² place it 55th for area. Density is about 61 people per square kilometre, fairly even compared with its Sahelian neighbours.

The capital in the game is Yaoundé, in the south of the country, inland. Many players answer Douala, the economic metropolis and big port on the Gulf of Guinea: that is the country’s typical mix-up, and the right answer is Yaoundé.

Cameroon has six neighbours, making it one of the best “connected” countries in Central Africa: Nigeria to the west, Chad to the north-east, the Central African Republic to the east, then the Congo, Gabon and Equatorial Guinea to the south. It is often described as “Africa in miniature”, because a few hundred kilometres take you from equatorial forest to savannah and the semi-arid north.

The country has two official languages, French and English, a legacy of the colonial split between two powers. Its flag of three vertical bands, green, red and yellow, carries a yellow star at the centre of the red band: a detail that sets it apart from neighbouring flags with the same palette.
