How it works

The principle fits in a sentence: a flag appears, you name the country among the options. What makes the difference is how the questions are drawn.

GeoG ranks all {{countries}} countries by familiarity — an internal measure of how often a country is known to the general public. So a run does not just pick at random: it mixes obvious flags with rare ones, so that a session stays playable without ever becoming pure recitation. The further you go, the larger the share of obscure countries.

Speed and accuracy both count towards the score, normalised on a 0–1000 scale shared by every mode in the game. Answering correctly in two seconds is worth more than answering correctly in ten.

Three ways to play it: solo, picking a continent to target one area; in the daily challenge, where every player faces the exact same run; or in an online duel, against a friend or a matched opponent.

Frequently asked

How many flags are in the game?
{{countries}} — the 193 UN member states plus the Holy See and Palestine. The reasoning is set out in our guide on how many countries there are.
Is the flag game free?
Entirely, and no account is needed for the daily challenge. It runs in the browser, or through the iPhone and Android app.
Can I practise one continent at a time?
Yes. Solo play lets you pick a zone: Europe, Africa, Asia, North America, South America or Oceania. It is the fastest way to fill a specific gap.
Which flags are hardest?
Those of the Oceanian and Caribbean micro-states, and the lookalike pairs — Chad and Romania, Monaco and Indonesia, Australia and New Zealand.
