Russia

The largest country in the world, filed under Europe by the game, with fourteen neighbours to recite.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

Russia is the first country in the world by area, with over 17 million square kilometres, about 1.1 times Canada, the second. Its population, 144 million according to the game, ranks it only ninth: at 8 people per square kilometre, its density is among the lowest of the large countries, most of the territory being thinly inhabited.

The game places Russia in Europe, as many rankings do, because most of its inhabitants and its capital are there. That is why the country appears on the European list although most of its territory, Siberia, lies in Asia. It is an excellent example of the difference between physical geography and a quiz's split.

The capital is Moscow, not Saint Petersburg, the imperial capital from 1712 to 1918. The Soviet government returned to Moscow in 1918, and it has stayed there since.

With fourteen playable land neighbours, Russia holds, with China, the highest count in the game: Norway, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Belarus, Ukraine, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, China, Mongolia and North Korea. You can recite them by going around the country from west to east, starting with Norway. Lithuania and Poland touch it only through the Kaliningrad exclave, cut off from the rest of the territory.
