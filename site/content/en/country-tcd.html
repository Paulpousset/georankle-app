Chad

The heart of Africa, between the Sahara and Lake Chad, and a flag that is the despair of quizzes.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Chad has 20.3 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 64th in the world, on 1,284,000 km², the 20th territory on the list. Density is about 16 people per square kilometre, but the population is very unevenly spread: the north is Saharan, and life concentrates in the south, around the Chari river and the capital.

The capital is N’Djamena, in the far west of the country, on the border with Cameroon. Chad is landlocked and has six neighbours: Libya to the north, Sudan to the east, the Central African Republic to the south, Cameroon and Nigeria to the south-west, Niger to the west. It gives its name to Lake Chad, which these countries share, and which has shrunk a great deal since the mid-twentieth century.

For geography, Chad is the country that links North Africa and Central Africa: to locate it, look for the country at the centre of the Sahel belt.

The flag is the big trap: three vertical bands of blue, yellow and red, almost identical to those of Romania, which even led to an official request. The only difference is the shade of blue. In a flag quiz, context is the key: if the game also offers an African country, it is Chad.
