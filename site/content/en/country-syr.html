Syria

Five neighbours in the heart of the Middle East and a red, white and black flag that several other countries share.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Syria has 24.7 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 57th in the world, over 185,180 km², the 87th largest territory. Density is about 133 people per square kilometre, concentrated in the west and along the Euphrates.

The capital is Damascus, in the south-west, a city reputed to be among the oldest continuously inhabited in the world. The country has a Mediterranean frontage on its west coast, narrow but real.

Syria has five neighbours: Türkiye to the north, Iraq to the east and south-east, Jordan to the south, Israel to the south-west and Lebanon to the west. It is therefore a crossroads between Anatolia, Mesopotamia and the Levant, and a good country to know for anchoring the whole region: once Syria is placed, its five neighbours follow at a glance.

The flag has three horizontal bands, red, white and black, with two green five-pointed stars on the white band. The same three colours, called pan-Arab, appear on the flags of Egypt, Iraq and Yemen: only the emblems change. For Syria it is the two green stars that make the difference, as the guide to similar flags explains.
