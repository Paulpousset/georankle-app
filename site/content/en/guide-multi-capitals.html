Countries with several capitals: twelve states with split institutions

When the government, parliament and courts are not in the same city, “what is the capital?” no longer has one answer. Here are twelve cases, and the answer the game keeps for each.

Guide · game data, November 2026

Among the {{countries}} countries in the game, most leave no doubt: one capital, one city, all the power in one place. But about a dozen states have split their institutions between two or more cities, or moved their capital without the historic metropolis losing its role. These are the ones that cost points in a quiz, because the city you name by instinct is not always the one that is expected. This page does not repeat the list of capital traps: it takes one subject, the split capital, and says for each country what the game answers.

Three ways to have several capitals

These twelve countries are not all alike. They fall into three families, which helps to remember them.

Powers shared between cities

Here each branch of power has its own city. South Africa is the textbook case: the executive in Pretoria, the legislature in Cape Town, the judiciary in Bloemfontein. Bolivia works on the same principle in a two-city version: Sucre is the constitutional capital, but the government and parliament sit in La Paz, higher and larger. Chile placed its Congress in Valparaíso, on the coast, while the rest of the government stays in Santiago. The game keeps Pretoria, Sucre and Santiago.

The constitution’s capital and the government’s

In the Netherlands, the constitution names Amsterdam as the capital, but the government, parliament and highest court sit in The Hague. The two cities are about fifty kilometres apart, and the trap is that the common answer, Amsterdam, is also strictly correct. In Malaysia, Kuala Lumpur remains the capital, but the federal administration has moved to Putrajaya, a city built for the purpose. Benin deserves its asterisk too: Porto-Novo is the official capital, while Cotonou concentrates activity.

The moved capital

Finally, some countries decided to leave their big city without that city ceasing to matter. Ivory Coast named Yamoussoukro, in the centre of the country, but Abidjan stayed the economic engine (see the country page). Tanzania likewise chose Dodoma, while Dar es Salaam remains by far the best-known city. Burundi is the most recent of the twelve: political power moved to Gitega, with Bujumbura remaining the economic capital. In these cases the right answer is the chosen city, rarely the famous one.

How to remember them

Remembering twelve exceptions is no harder than remembering one, provided you sort them. One method that works: learn the three long-named capitals first (Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, Yamoussoukro, Porto-Novo), whose unfamiliar names are enough to signal a trap. Then, for each country, memorise a pair, “expected city / famous city”, rather than two isolated names: Sucre/La Paz, Pretoria/Cape Town, Dodoma/Dar es Salaam. Finally, ask the question the right way round in a quiz: not “where is the power?” but “which city does the official list give?”.

This list does not cover everything. A few countries have a disputed capital, such as Israel; that subject belongs in the guide to disputed territories, because the question there is political, not administrative.

Frequently asked questions

Which country has three capitals?
South Africa: Pretoria for the executive, Cape Town for the legislature and Bloemfontein for the judiciary. The game keeps Pretoria.
What is the capital of Bolivia, Sucre or La Paz?
Both, depending on the criterion. Sucre is the constitutional capital, La Paz the seat of government. In the game the expected answer is Sucre.
Is Amsterdam really the capital of the Netherlands?
Yes, it is the capital named in the constitution. The government and parliament, however, sit in The Hague.
Why is Yamoussoukro the capital of Ivory Coast rather than Abidjan?
The move of the capital to the centre of the country was decided in 1983, but Abidjan remained the economic metropolis. For a quiz, the answer is Yamoussoukro.
