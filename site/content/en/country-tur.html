Turkey

The capital is not Istanbul: the most classic trap in capital-city geography.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Turkey has 86 million people according to the game, eighteenth in the world, over 785,000 km² (thirty-sixth by area). Density is 109 people per square kilometre. Its official name has been Türkiye since 2022, a change covered in our guide to countries that changed their name.

The capital is Ankara, not Istanbul, which is nonetheless the largest city and the former capital of the Ottoman Empire. Ankara was chosen in 1923 by Mustafa Kemal Atatürk when the Republic was founded, in the heart of Anatolia, away from the straits. It is one of the most common mistakes in capital-city geography.

The country has eight land neighbours: Greece and Bulgaria in Europe; Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Iraq and Syria in Asia. It straddles two continents, separated by the Bosphorus strait: a small part, around Istanbul, lies in Europe, the vast majority in Asia. The game files it under Western Asia.

The flag is red, with a white crescent and star. That is all there is to remember, but it must be told apart from Tunisia’s, which shows a red crescent and star inside a white disc in the middle of a red field.
