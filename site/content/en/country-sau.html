Saudi Arabia

Twelfth in the world by area, forty-fifth by population.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Saudi Arabia is one of the countries where the gap between size and population is most striking. At 2,149,690 km² it is twelfth in the world by area; with 35.3 million people it is only forty-fifth by population. Density falls to about 16 people per square kilometre: desert covers most of the territory, and people cluster in a few regions around Riyadh, the Red Sea coast and the Gulf.

The capital is Riyadh, in the middle of the country and far from the sea. It is also the largest city. In a quiz, the cities people confuse with it are Mecca and Jeddah, the latter being the great Red Sea port.

The game counts seven land neighbours, which makes it a good borders exercise. Jordan, Iraq and Kuwait follow one another to the north; Qatar and the United Arab Emirates sit on the Gulf; Oman lies to the south-east and Yemen to the south. The country fills most of the Arabian Peninsula, flanked by the Red Sea to the west and the Persian Gulf to the east: remember the big “body” of the country between the two coasts and the layout follows.

The flag is plain green with a white Arabic inscription, the declaration of faith, and a sword underneath. Because it carries text it cannot be confused with any other green flag, but it is among the hardest to describe from memory: think “green, white script, sword”.
