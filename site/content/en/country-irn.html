Iran

Seven borders, two seas, and an official name only ninety years old.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Iran has 92 million people according to the game, seventeenth in the world, and its 1.75 million km² give it seventeenth place by area too, a rare coincidence. Its density, 52 people per square kilometre, reflects a central plateau that is largely arid, ringed by mountain chains.

The capital is Tehran, at the foot of the Alborz mountains, and also the largest city in the country. Abroad, the country was long known as Persia; the name Iran became official in international relations in 1935.

Iran has seven land neighbours: Turkey, Armenia and Azerbaijan to the north-west, Turkmenistan to the north-east, Afghanistan and Pakistan to the east, Iraq to the west. It has two coastlines, in the north on the Caspian Sea, in the south on the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman. Note that the game files it under the “Southern Asia” sub-region, not Western Asia as you might expect.

The flag is a horizontal tricolour of green, white and red, with a red emblem in the centre. Hungary uses the same three colours, but in the opposite order (red, white, green) and with no emblem.
