South America capitals quiz: 12 countries, 3 blocks and the cities people mix up

Twelve capitals is not many. Nearly every mistake comes from the same source: the famous big city that is not the capital.

Guide · game data, October 2026

The game counts {{count:amerique-du-sud}} countries in South America, so twelve capitals. The annotated list, including the Sucre versus La Paz debate, is in the atlas of South American capitals. This page does something else: it splits the continent into three blocks that are easy to recite, shows where a quiz hides its traps, and gives the order in which to revise so you stop hesitating.

One continent, one giant and eleven other countries

First, a sense of scale. According to the game’s data, the twelve countries add up to about 435 million people on 17.7 million km². Brazil alone accounts for nearly half: 49% of the population and 48% of the area. It touches all its neighbours except two, Chile and Ecuador. That helps in a quiz: when a question is about an unfamiliar country in the interior, Brazil is almost always next door.

Three blocks for twelve capitals

Rather than reciting the list alphabetically, split it along the map. Within each block the countries below are ordered geographically, north to south or Pacific to Atlantic.

The Andean and Caribbean block: five countries

Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and Bolivia make up the north-western arc of the continent. Four of these capitals have short Spanish names (Caracas, Bogotá, Quito, Lima). The fifth is Bolivia, which needs one more sentence.

The Southern Cone: four countries

Chile, Argentina, Uruguay and Paraguay occupy the tip of the continent. Their capitals are the easiest to place, because they are also the biggest cities in their countries. The one trap is the spelling of Asunción, the capital of Paraguay, which many players write without the accent.

The Atlantic block: three countries

Brazil and the two small Guianas of the game, Guyana and Suriname. Brasília is the only capital on the continent built from scratch in the twentieth century. Georgetown and Paramaribo are the only two capitals where the language is neither Spanish nor Portuguese: English in Guyana, Dutch in Suriname. French Guiana, a French territory, is not a country in the game and so has no capital there.

The big cities that are not capitals

This is where points are lost. Four cities keep turning up in wrong answers:

  • Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo for Brazil. Rio was the capital until 1960; São Paulo is the largest city. The answer is Brasília.
  • Guayaquil for Ecuador. It is the most populous city in the country, but the capital is Quito, in the Andes.
  • Santa Cruz de la Sierra for Bolivia. It is larger than Sucre and La Paz, and claims neither title.
  • Valparaíso for Chile. Congress sits there, but the capital remains Santiago.

The practical rule: on this continent, nine capitals out of twelve are the largest city in their country. If a question offers a famous city that is not in the lists above, check the three exceptions first: Brazil, Ecuador and Bolivia.

A revision plan in two sessions

  1. Session 1: the Southern Cone and the Atlantic block. Seven countries. Start with Santiago, Buenos Aires, Montevideo and Asunción, then add Brasília, Georgetown and Paramaribo.
  2. Session 2: the Andean block. Five countries, from Venezuela to Bolivia. Finish with a mixed run of all twelve, saying the rule “Brazil, Ecuador, Bolivia” out loud before each run.

Two sessions are enough, two or three days apart, following the rhythm suggested in the study guide. Once the twelve capitals are secure, the next step is to place them on the map, which is what the Globe mode is for.

Frequently asked questions

How many capitals does South America have in the game?
{{count:amerique-du-sud}}, one per country. French Guiana, a French territory, is not counted.
What is the capital of Brazil?
Brasília, inaugurated in 1960. Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo are better known but are not capitals.
Sucre or La Paz for Bolivia?
The game uses Sucre, the constitutional capital. La Paz houses the government and parliament, which is why many sources give both.
What is the capital of Ecuador?
Quito. Guayaquil is the most populous city in the country, which makes it the most common wrong answer.
