Angola

A country whose province Cabinda is cut off from the rest by another state.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Angola has 37.9 million people according to the game, fortieth in the world, but its 1,246,700 km² rank twenty-second by area. That is only 30 people per square kilometre: the country is vast, populated mostly on the coast and on the interior highlands.

The capital is Luanda, on the Atlantic, also the largest city. There is no capital trap, but a language trap: Angola is Portuguese-speaking, like Mozambique, and its landmarks are Portuguese, which sets it apart from most of its neighbours.

The game lists four land neighbours: Namibia to the south, Zambia to the east, and two countries with similar names to the north, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and the Republic of the Congo. Cabinda, an oil-rich Angolan province, is an exclave north of the mouth of the Congo River: a thin strip of the DR Congo separates it from the rest of the country. It is the kind of detail that makes a good borders question.

The flag is divided into two horizontal bands, red above and black below, with a yellow emblem in the centre made of half a cogwheel, a machete and a star. It recalls Mozambique’s by its emblem, though not by its colours.
