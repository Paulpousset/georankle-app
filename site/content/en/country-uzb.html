Uzbekistan

The most populous country in Central Asia, and one of only two in the world that are doubly landlocked.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Uzbekistan has 36.4 million people according to the game, forty-third in the world. It is by far the most populous country of Central Asia: Kazakhstan, though more than six times larger, has only 20.6 million. At 448,920 km², Uzbekistan ranks fifty-seventh by area, which gives a density of about 81 people per square kilometre.

The capital is Tashkent, also the largest city, in the north-east. The names that crop up in travel stories, Samarkand, Bukhara and Khiva, are Silk Road cities rather than capitals, which makes them good decoys in a quiz on Asian capitals.

The country has no sea coast, as the game flags, and it has an extra twist: none of its five neighbours touches an ocean either. Reaching open water means crossing two borders, hence the term doubly landlocked. The neighbours are Kazakhstan to the north, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan to the east and south-east, Afghanistan to the south and Turkmenistan to the south-west. All five names end in “-stan”, which makes the list easy to scramble, exactly the kind of series the Borders mode is built to fix.

One more landmark: the Aral Sea, now much reduced, lies partly in the north-west of the country, on the border with Kazakhstan. The flag has blue, white and green bands separated by thin red lines, with a crescent and stars in the upper-left corner.
