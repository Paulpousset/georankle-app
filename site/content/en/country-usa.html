United States

A capital people give to New York by reflex, and only two neighbours for the world's third-largest country.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

In the game's data the United States is both the third country in the world by population (340 million people) and by area. That is a rare position: most giants are big in only one way. China and India have more people; Russia and Canada have more land.

The capital is Washington, or Washington, D.C. (“District of Columbia”). It is not New York, which people name by reflex, nor Los Angeles. The city was founded around 1800 on neutral ground outside any state, precisely so that the capital would depend on none of them. Do not confuse it either with Washington State, at the other end of the country on the Pacific coast.

On the map this huge country has only two land neighbours: Canada to the north and Mexico to the south. That is few for such a territory, and it makes it an easy starting point for a review of North American borders. Alaska, cut off from the rest of the territory by Canada, and Hawaii, in the middle of the Pacific, change nothing in the count.

Density, finally, is modest: about 35 people per square kilometre, where India has more than four hundred. An easy fact to remember: the United States weighs heavily in people, but above all it is very large open space.
