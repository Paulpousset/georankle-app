Niger

Seven neighbours, no coast, and a name constantly mixed up with Nigeria’s.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Niger has 27 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 55th in the world, over 1,267,000 km², the 21st largest territory on the planet. Density is only about 21 people per square kilometre, because most of the north belongs to the Sahara: the population is concentrated in the southern strip.

The capital is Niamey, in the west, on the river that gives the country its name. Niger is landlocked and has seven neighbours: Algeria and Libya to the north, Chad to the east, Nigeria and Benin to the south, then Burkina Faso and Mali to the west. It is one of the African countries with the most neighbours.

The mix-up with Nigeria is the most frequent trap: both names come from the same river, and the two countries touch. Nigeria is in the south with an Atlantic coast and is the most populous in Africa; Niger is in the north, landlocked, much larger in area but far less populated. See the Nigeria profile.

The flag has three horizontal bands of orange, white and green, with an orange disc at the centre of the white band. Without the disc it would resemble the flag of Ireland or Ivory Coast.
