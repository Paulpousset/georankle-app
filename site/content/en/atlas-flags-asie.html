The flags of Asia

The continent of central symbols: a disc, a crescent, a wheel, a leaf. The backgrounds resemble each other, the emblems settle it.

Reference · {{count:asie}} flags · game data, updated August 2026

Where Europe plays on bands and Africa on a shared palette, Asia bets on the central symbol. That makes its flags paradoxically easy: one emblem, one country, or nearly.

The discs

Four countries place a disc at the centre of their flag, and they make four classic confusions. Japan: red disc centred on white. Bangladesh: red disc slightly offset towards the hoist, on green. Palau — technically in Oceania — : yellow disc offset on light blue. Laos: white disc centred on a blue band framed in red.

The offset towards the hoist is not accidental: it compensates for the optical effect of a flag in the wind, so the disc looks centred.

The crescents

A crescent and star nearly always signal a country of Muslim tradition: Turkey, Pakistan, Malaysia, the Maldives, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Azerbaijan. Telling them apart means looking at the background and the details — the Turkish crescent stands alone on red, the Pakistani one comes with a white vertical band, the Malaysian one sits above fourteen stripes for the states of the federation.

The flag that is not a rectangle

Nepal is the only country in the world whose flag is not a quadrilateral: two stacked triangular pennants, carrying a moon and a sun. Its geometric construction is described formula by formula in the Nepalese constitution. It is the most recognisable flag on the planet — and a free answer in any quiz.

Worth noting too: Cyprus, one of only two flags in the world depicting a map of its own country.

Frequently asked

Which is the only non-rectangular flag in the world?
Nepal’s: two stacked triangular pennants carrying a moon and a sun. Its geometry is defined mathematically in the country’s constitution.
How do you tell Japan from Bangladesh?
The background and the position. Japan: red disc perfectly centred on white. Bangladesh: red disc slightly offset towards the hoist, on green.
Is Turkey in Europe or Asia?
It straddles both. In the split used here, Turkey is filed under Asia, where nearly all of its territory lies. Its capital is Ankara.
