Ethiopia

A high-altitude capital, six neighbours, and a landlocked country that became a demographic giant.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

Ethiopia is the tenth country in the world by population, with 132 million people according to the game, and the second in Africa behind Nigeria. Its area of 1.14 million km² ranks it twenty-sixth. Its density, 116 people per square kilometre, is close to China's.

The capital is Addis Ababa, whose name means “new flower” in Amharic. It is also the largest city, but it has a special importance: it hosts the seat of the African Union. That makes it one of the continent's diplomatic capitals, which helps to remember it. Spellings: Addis Ababa in English, Addis-Abeba in French.

Ethiopia is landlocked: it has had no access to the sea since Eritrea's independence in 1993. It has six land neighbours: Sudan and South Sudan to the west, Eritrea to the north, Djibouti and Somalia to the east, and Kenya to the south. Tiny Djibouti is the sea gateway for much of its trade. It is a good example of how a landlocked country depends on a neighbour.

As for the flag, Ethiopia's has three horizontal stripes, green, yellow, red, with a blue emblem in the centre. These three colours, called pan-African, inspired the flags of many African countries.
