The capitals of Asia

This is the continent where capitals move most — in name as in location. A list learned ten years ago already contains errors.

Reference · {{count:asie}} countries · game data, updated August 2026

Asia has {{count:asie}} countries in the split used here, Turkey and Kazakhstan included — two states straddling two continents, filed on the side where most of their territory lies. It is also the most unstable continent for capitals: three of them have changed name or location within a generation.

The capitals that moved

Myanmar transferred its seat from Yangon to Naypyidaw in 2005, a city raised from nothing in the centre of the country, whose twenty-lane motorways remain almost empty. Kazakhstan moved its capital from Almaty northwards in 1997, then renamed the city three times: Akmola, Astana, Nur-Sultan in 2019, and Astana again in 2022. It is a formidable quiz trap, because the right answer depends on the year the quiz was written.

Sri Lanka has the longest capital name in the world: Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, a suburb of Colombo and seat of parliament since 1982. Colombo remains the commercial capital, and the answer everybody gives.

The three most common traps

Turkey: Ankara, not Istanbul. The move dates from 1923 and the founding of the Republic — Istanbul stayed the largest city, and by far the most visited.

India: New Delhi, a district inside the Delhi conurbation. Not Mumbai, and not Kolkata, which was nonetheless the capital of British India until 1911.

China: Beijing, not Shanghai — the latter is more populous and holds the finance, the former has held political power since the Ming.

Frequently asked

What is the capital of Kazakhstan?
Astana. The city was called Akmola, then Astana, then Nur-Sultan from 2019 to 2022, before taking the name Astana back. It is the capital that has changed name most this century.
Ankara or Istanbul?
Ankara, capital of Turkey since 1923. Istanbul is the largest city and the economic centre, but has not been the capital since the end of the Ottoman Empire.
Which capital has the longest name?
Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, in Sri Lanka — seat of parliament since 1982, in the suburbs of Colombo.
