Yemen

In the south-west of the Arabian Peninsula, facing the Horn of Africa.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Yemen has 40.6 million people according to the game, thirty-eighth in the world, on 527,970 km², fifty-first by area. The density is about 77 people per square kilometre, with the population concentrated in the western highlands, far from the desert that fills the east and north.

The capital is Sana’a, which the game spells with an apostrophe. It lies in the mountains, at over 2,000 metres. The trap is Aden, the big southern port, often given by mistake. The present country was formed in 1990 by the union of North Yemen and South Yemen.

The game lists only two land neighbours: Saudi Arabia to the north and Oman to the east. The country opens on the Red Sea to the west and the Gulf of Aden to the south. It also controls Socotra, an isolated island in the Indian Ocean, closer to Somalia than to the Yemeni mainland.

The flag is a horizontal tricolour of red, white and black with no symbol at all. It is almost impossible to tell from Egypt’s or Syria’s without an emblem: the safest way is to link it to its position, at the tip of the Arabian Peninsula.
