Nepal

Two giant neighbours, peaks above eight thousand metres, and the only national flag in the world that is not a rectangle.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Nepal has 29.7 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 51st in the world, but only 147,180 km², which puts it 93rd for area. The result is a density of about 201 people per square kilometre, very high for a country that is largely mountainous.

The capital is Kathmandu, in a valley in the centre of the country. Nepal is landlocked and has only two neighbours, both giants: India to the south, east and west, and China to the north, across the Himalayas. That buffer position between the two most populous countries in the world is what defines Nepal on a map: a narrow elongated strip wedged between two immense masses, easy to find once you have located India and China.

Nepal holds part of the Himalayan range, including Mount Everest, which stands on the border with China. The relief shapes trade and settlement: the southern plains, close to India, are the most densely populated.

Its flag is the only non-rectangular national flag: two stacked triangular pennants, crimson with a blue border, carrying a white sun and moon. It is the easiest flag in the game to recognise, even for someone who has never studied a single profile.
