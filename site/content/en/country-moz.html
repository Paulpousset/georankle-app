Mozambique

A long Indian Ocean coastline and a flag like no other.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Mozambique has 34.6 million people according to the game, forty-sixth in the world. Its 799,380 km² are the thirty-fourth largest territory, for a density of about 43 people per square kilometre. The country is shaped like a long crescent facing east, running along the Indian Ocean for its whole length.

The capital is Maputo, at the far south near the South African border. It is also the largest city, and was called Lourenço Marques in colonial times. Like Angola, Mozambique is Portuguese-speaking, which sets it apart from nearly all its neighbours.

The game gives it six land neighbours. Tanzania lies to the north; Malawi, Zambia and Zimbabwe to the west; South Africa and Eswatini to the south-west. Malawi pushes into Mozambican territory like a finger, and that shape detail is what helps to place it. To the east, the Mozambique Channel separates the country from Madagascar, the island that shows up on every map of the region.

The flag is a rarity. Three horizontal bands in green, black and yellow, separated by thin white lines, a red triangle at the hoist, and in that triangle a yellow star carrying a book, a hoe and an assault rifle. It is one of the very few national flags to show a modern rifle, which makes it a near-certain quiz answer: a rifle resting on a book leaves no doubt. It belongs to the ten flags with no equivalent in African flag families.
