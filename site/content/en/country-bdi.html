Burundi

Rwanda’s southern twin changed capital in 2019: the expected answer is no longer the big city on the lake.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Burundi has a little over 14 million inhabitants according to the game data, 77th in the world, just behind Rwanda, and 30th in Africa. Its 27,830 km² make it the 142nd territory in the game and the tenth-smallest in Africa. Density exceeds 500 people per square kilometre: third on the continent, behind Mauritius and Rwanda.

The capital is Gitega, in the centre of the country, since a law passed in 2019. Before that date, and in most school memories, it was Bujumbura, on the shore of Lake Tanganyika, which remains the largest city and the economic capital. The quiz expects Gitega: it is one of the cases, with Astana or Naypyidaw, where the right answer changed recently.

The country is landlocked and has only three neighbours: Rwanda to the north, Tanzania to the east and south, the Democratic Republic of the Congo to the west. Lake Tanganyika lines the whole south-west and is the landmark to look for on the globe: Burundi sits at the northern tip of that long lake. One of its tributaries, in the south of the country, is often presented as the most distant source of the Nile.

The flag looks like no other on the continent: a white saltire, that is a diagonal cross, separates two red triangles at the top and bottom from two green triangles at the sides, with a white disc in the centre bearing three red six-pointed stars.
