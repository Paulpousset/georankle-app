United Kingdom

One land neighbour, a modest surface and a flag that became the template for several others.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

The United Kingdom has 69.3 million people according to the game, twenty-first in the world, just ahead of Tanzania and France. Its 243,610 km² rank only seventy-eighth by area: the country is small and therefore dense, with 284 people per square kilometre, well above France or Spain.

The capital is London and nobody hesitates on that. The trap is in the name of the country. “United Kingdom”, “Great Britain” and “England” are not the same thing: England is only one of four nations, Great Britain is the island that carries England, Scotland and Wales, and the United Kingdom adds Northern Ireland. The game uses the United Kingdom, the sovereign state.

It has a single land neighbour: Ireland, which shares the border with Northern Ireland on the island of Ireland. That is the lowest possible count for a country that has any, and a textbook case for the Borders mode: every other limit is maritime, from the Channel to the North Sea.

The flag, the Union Jack, stacks the crosses of Saint George (England), Saint Andrew (Scotland) and Saint Patrick (Ireland). Wales does not appear on it. The design became a template: it sits in the upper corner of the Australian and New Zealand flags, which is why those two are mistaken for each other more often than for the British one.
