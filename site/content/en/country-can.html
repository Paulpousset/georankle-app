Canada

Second largest country in the world, with a single land neighbour.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Canada has 41.3 million people according to the game, thirty-seventh in the world, but its 15,634,410 km² make it second in the world by area, behind Russia. The density is 2.6 people per square kilometre, one of the lowest of any country: the great majority of the population lives in a narrow belt near the southern border.

The capital is Ottawa, a quiz classic: not Toronto, the largest city, nor Montréal, nor Vancouver. Ottawa sits on the boundary of Ontario and Quebec, on the Ottawa River. The choice was a historical compromise between the English-speaking and French-speaking communities.

The game lists a single land neighbour: the United States, to the south and in the north-west (through Alaska). That border is the longest in the world between two countries. Canada also has the longest coastline in the world, on three oceans, the Atlantic, the Pacific and the Arctic, which makes it easy to spot on the globe.

The flag is red and white with a stylised red maple leaf in the centre. It is one of the most recognised flags in the world, and one of the few to carry a leaf.
