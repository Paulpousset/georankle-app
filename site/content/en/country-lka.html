Sri Lanka

An island with no land neighbour, a capital with an endless name and a big city right next to it: Colombo.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Sri Lanka has 21.9 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 60th in the world, but only 65,610 km², the 122nd largest territory. Density reaches about 334 people per square kilometre, one of the highest in the game for a country of this size.

The capital in the game is Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, with a name so long that players almost always answer Colombo, the neighbouring big city, economic centre and former capital. The two cities almost touch: Kotte is a suburb of Colombo, and how institutions are split between them is detailed in the guide to countries with several capitals.

Sri Lanka is an island south-east of India, separated from it by a narrow strait: it therefore has no land neighbour in the game. It lies in Southern Asia, on the great shipping routes of the Indian Ocean, which explains its role as a trading crossroads for centuries.

The country was called Ceylon until it took its current name in 1972, as Ceylon tea still recalls. Its flag is the only one of its kind: a golden lion holding a sword on a crimson field, alongside two bands of green and orange.
