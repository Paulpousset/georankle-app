Egypt

A million km² and one river: why Egypt’s average density says almost nothing.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Egypt has 117 million people according to the game, thirteenth in the world and third in Africa after Nigeria and Ethiopia. Its territory passes a million km² (twenty-ninth), but the average density of 116 people per square kilometre is misleading: the overwhelming majority of the country is desert, and the population is packed into the narrow Nile valley and its delta.

The capital is Cairo, also the largest city in the country and one of the largest urban areas in Africa. It sits where the Nile fans out into its delta, which has made the site strategic for centuries.

The game counts four land neighbours: Libya to the west, Sudan to the south, and to the east Israel and Palestine, around the Sinai peninsula. Egypt is one of the few countries spanning two continents: Sinai belongs to Asia, the rest to Africa, and the Suez Canal links the Mediterranean to the Red Sea.

The flag has three horizontal bands of red, white and black, with a golden eagle in the middle. The eagle is enough to tell it from Yemen’s flag, which has the same three bands and no emblem at all.
