China

Fourteen neighbours, a capital with no trap, and the world's second-largest population.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

China ranks second in the world for population, with 1.41 billion people according to the game's data, just behind India. By area it is fourth, behind Russia, Canada and the United States. The gap with the United States is so small that players often dispute it: the game puts the United States third.

Its capital, Beijing (Pékin in French), is hardly a trap: it is neither Shanghai, the largest city for business, nor Hong Kong, a special administrative region. The confusion is spelling rather than geography: “Beijing”, “Pékin” in French and “Peking” in older texts all name the same city.

It is through its borders that China impresses. The game gives it fourteen playable land neighbours: Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Afghanistan, Pakistan, India, Nepal, Bhutan, Laos, Mongolia, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia and Vietnam. That is the highest count in the game, tied with Russia. The table below gives the whole set, from Kazakhstan to Vietnam.

One last landmark: at about 147 people per square kilometre, China is far less dense than India, though the two have similar populations. A large part of its territory, in the west, is almost empty.
