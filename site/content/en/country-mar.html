Morocco

Two seas, a strait and a capital that is not the largest city.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Morocco has 38.1 million people according to the game, thirty-ninth in the world, on 446,550 km², fifty-eighth by area. The density is about 85 people per square kilometre, with the population concentrated on the Atlantic side, between Casablanca and Rabat.

The capital is Rabat, and it is a classic trap: Casablanca is bigger, Marrakesh more famous, but the capital is Rabat, on the Atlantic. In a four-choice quiz, Casablanca is almost always among the wrong answers.

The game lists three land neighbours: Algeria to the east, Mauritania to the south and Spain, through the enclaves of Ceuta and Melilla. Western Sahara, whose status is disputed, is not a country in the game; the question is covered in the guide to disputed territories. Morocco is separated from Europe only by the Strait of Gibraltar, about fourteen kilometres wide at its narrowest.

The flag is entirely red, with a green pentagram in the centre. It is one of the simplest flags in the Arab world, and one of the few without a stripe or a crescent. The Atlas range, which crosses the country from south-west to north-east, helps picture it.
