Bangladesh

The world's eighth-largest population on a territory the size of a large European country.

Country profile · game data, September 2026

Bangladesh has 174 million people according to the game, making it the eighth country in the world by population. Yet it covers barely 147,570 km², ninety-second by area: that gives a density of about 1,176 people per square kilometre, the highest of any large country. For comparison, that is more than two and a half times India's density.

The capital is Dhaka, written Dacca in French. No capital trap here: Dhaka is also the largest city. The difficulty lies elsewhere, in history: the country was born in 1971, when East Pakistan broke away from Pakistan. Before that it was East Bengal in British India, then a province of Pakistan from 1947 to 1971.

Bangladesh has only two land neighbours: India, which surrounds it almost entirely, and Myanmar to the south-east. On a map it is recognisable by its shape, like a country carved into India's flank, and by the huge delta of the Ganges and Brahmaputra opening onto the Bay of Bengal in the south.

As for the flag, it is one of the easiest to remember and one of the easiest to mix up: a red disc on a green field. The disc is set slightly toward the hoist, which distinguishes it from Japan's flag, red on white.
