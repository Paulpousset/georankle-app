Tanzania

The capital is not the big coastal city, and the neighbours are so many that one is always forgotten.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Tanzania has 68.6 million people according to the game, twenty-second in the world. It leads France by fewer than ten thousand people: on the scale of their populations the two are almost level. With 947,300 km² it ranks thirtieth by area, and its density of 72 people per square kilometre is middling.

The capital is Dodoma, in the middle of the country, and that is the answer that costs points. Most players say Dar es Salaam, the big port on the Indian Ocean, long the seat of government and still the leading economic city. The official move to Dodoma, meant to bring the capital closer to the geographic centre, is one more of the relocated African capitals: see the list of African capitals.

Tanzania has eight land neighbours: Kenya and Uganda to the north, Rwanda, Burundi and DR Congo to the west, Zambia and Malawi to the south-west, Mozambique to the south. Few countries have as many, and it touches East, Central and Southern Africa at once. Three of Africa’s great lakes, Victoria, Tanganyika and Malawi, lie partly within its borders.

The country was born in 1964 from the union of Tanganyika, on the mainland, and Zanzibar, an archipelago in the Indian Ocean, hence the name. Its flag is crossed by a black band edged in yellow, running from the lower to the upper corner between a green and a blue triangle. It resembles no other, which makes it an easy flag once seen.
