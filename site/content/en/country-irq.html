Iraq

The land of two rivers, not to be confused with its neighbour Iran.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Iraq has 46.0 million people according to the game, thirty-fourth in the world, on 435,050 km², fifty-ninth by area. The density of 106 people per square kilometre hides a very uneven spread: people live along the Tigris and Euphrates, far less in the western desert.

The capital is Baghdad, on the Tigris, also the largest city. There is no capital trap, but a name trap: Iraq and Iran differ by a single letter, and their capitals, Baghdad and Tehran, are easier to learn together. Iraq is to the west, Iran to the east; one is mostly Arab, the other Persian.

The game lists six land neighbours: Turkey to the north, Syria and Jordan to the west, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait to the south, Iran to the east. The country reaches the Persian Gulf only through a narrow strip, at the head of the Gulf between Kuwait and Iran. The Tigris and Euphrates cross it from north-west to south-east: this is ancient Mesopotamia, the “land between the rivers”.

The flag has three horizontal bands of red, white and black, with a green inscription in Arabic on the white band. It belongs to the family of pan-Arab colours, like those of Egypt, Syria or Yemen.
