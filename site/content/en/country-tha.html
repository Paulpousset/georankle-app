Thailand

A country shaped like an elephant’s head, four neighbours, and a flag whose twin is in Central America.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Thailand has 72 million people according to the game: twentieth in the world. Its 513,000 km² (fifty-second by area) give a density of 140 people per square kilometre. It was called Siam until 1939 and is notable for never having been colonised by a European power.

The capital is Bangkok, also by far the largest city in the country. Its full ceremonial name is among the longest in the world; Thais simply say Krung Thep. No trap here: the capital is the name you would expect.

The country has four land neighbours: Myanmar to the west and north, Laos to the north-east, Cambodia to the east, Malaysia to the south, along the peninsula. Thailand’s map looks like an elephant’s head whose trunk is the narrow peninsula running down towards Malaysia.

The flag has five horizontal stripes: red, white, blue, white, red, with the blue stripe twice as wide. Costa Rica’s is its twin: same colours, but with the wide stripe red and the outer ones blue. When in doubt, remember: blue in the middle for Thailand.
