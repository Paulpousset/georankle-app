Senegal

The western tip of the continent, and a country split in two by The Gambia.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Senegal has 18.5 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 68th in the world, on 196,710 km², the 86th territory on the list. Density is about 94 people per square kilometre, higher than most of its Sahel neighbours, with a population massed on the coast and around the Cape Verde peninsula.

The capital is Dakar, on that peninsula, the westernmost point of mainland Africa. Senegal has five neighbours: Mauritania to the north, Mali to the east, Guinea and Guinea-Bissau to the south, and The Gambia, which pushes in like a corridor inside the country, along the river of the same name.

The Gambia is the particularity that makes Senegal’s outline one of the easiest to recognise: the country wraps around its neighbour on three sides, and almost cuts the Casamance region, in the south, in two. To remember it, think of a “finger in a mitten”.

The flag has three vertical bands, green, yellow and red, with a green star in the centre of the yellow band. It is exactly Mali’s, with the star: a simple way to keep the difference in mind. Both countries carry these pan-African colours in the same order, green at the hoist.
