Chile

Over 4,000 km long, a few hundred wide: the most stretched country on the map.

Country profile · game data, November 2026

Chile has 19.8 million inhabitants according to the game, ranking 65th in the world, on 757,245 km², the 37th territory on the list. Density is about 26 people per square kilometre, but the average misleads: the population is concentrated around the capital, in the centre of the country, while the desert north and the icy south are almost empty.

The capital is Santiago, at the foot of the Andes. Chile has three neighbours in the game: Peru to the north, Bolivia to the north-east, and Argentina, which borders all the rest of its land frontier, along the Andes cordillera. The country is coastal, with the Pacific as its only seaboard.

Its shape is its signature: more than 4,000 km from north to south, for a very modest width. To place it on the globe, look for the thin strip running along the west of South America. In the north it touches the Atacama, one of the driest deserts on the planet, in the south, Patagonia and Tierra del Fuego shared with Argentina.

The flag has two horizontal bands, white above and red below, with a blue square holding a white star in the upper corner at the hoist. It is confused with Texas’s, which resembles it.
