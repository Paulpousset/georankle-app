Vietnam

A long, narrow country, a capital that is not the biggest city, and a flag that looks like its neighbour’s.

Country profile · game data, October 2026

Vietnam has 101 million people according to the game: sixteenth in the world, and the last on the list to pass 100 million. Its 331,000 km² (sixty-sixth by area) are narrow and very long, giving a density of 305 people per square kilometre, higher than neighbouring China’s.

The capital is Hanoi, in the north. It is not the largest city, a title that goes to Ho Chi Minh City in the south, the former Saigon: a classic small capital trap. Hanoi was the capital of the North and Saigon, the former name of Ho Chi Minh City, that of the South, until reunification in 1976.

Vietnam has three land neighbours: China to the north, Laos and Cambodia to the west. Its coast stretches along the South China Sea and draws an S shape that is instantly recognisable on a map.

The flag is red, with one large five-pointed yellow star in the centre. China also has a red field and yellow stars, but five of them, one large and four small, grouped in the top corner. Remember: a single big centred star for Vietnam, a cluster of stars at the top left for China.
