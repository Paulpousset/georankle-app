# Story-map wildlife models

All 3D animals used by `render_critters.py` are by **Quaternius**
(https://quaternius.com), released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain):
no attribution required, commercial use allowed. Credited here anyway.

Packs: Ultimate Animated Animals, Animated Dinosaurs, Easy Animated Enemies,
Animated Fish, Cute Fish, Ultimate Space Kit, Ultimate Spaceships.

GLBs are fetched from the public mirror
https://github.com/trebeljahr/quaternius-showcase (`public/glb/…`) by
`fetch_critters.mjs` into `critters/glb/` (git-ignored). Palettes, toon
shading, outline and camera are ours (critters.json + render_critters.py).
